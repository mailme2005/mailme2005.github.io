Privacy policy

Your garden stays on your device.

Little Garden is an offline garden game. The app does not collect or upload personal data and contains no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics. This policy also explains what happens when you contact us or visit this website.

Who this policy covers

This policy covers Little Garden: Mochi Bunny (一方小园; called Little Garden in the game) and this privacy and support website. The developer and operator is Guan Jie Tan. You can contact the support email below with privacy questions.

Records inside the game

Your garden layout, bunny name and habits, garden name, sound and language preferences, and postcards are stored on your device. No account or sign-in is needed. There is no developer-provided cloud sync, online community, or server receiving game data.

The app does not send these records to the developer. iOS system backups may include app data, depending on your settings and Apple services.

Photos permission and sharing

The app asks to add images to Photos only when you choose Save to Photos. It does not read your photo library. You can keep postcards in the in-app album without granting permission and can change Photos permission in iOS Settings.

When you use the system share sheet, you choose the recipient or app. A postcard may include the garden name you entered and the capture date. Recipients handle shared content under their own privacy practices.

Keeping and deleting local records

Local records remain until you delete the relevant content, start a new garden, or delete the app. You can remove postcards from the garden album or reset the garden using the in-game restart feature. Starting a new garden does not delete the separate postcard album or reset sound and language preferences.

Deleting the app removes its local data. Exported photos, shared content, and system backups must be managed separately. The developer has no copy of your garden and cannot remotely view, delete, or recover these local records.

When you send a support email

If you choose to email us, the support mailbox receives your email address, subject, message, and any images or files you attach. This information is used only to answer questions, troubleshoot issues, and handle privacy requests, not for advertising or tracking. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary personal information.

The support mailbox uses Gmail, where Google processes messages under its service and privacy rules. Emails and attachments are kept only as long as needed to handle the inquiry, follow up on related issues, or meet applicable legal obligations, and are deleted when no longer needed.

You can request access, correction, or deletion of your support emails by writing from the original contact address. We may need to confirm control of that address to protect the information. If records must be retained by law, we will explain why. Backup copies held by the email provider follow its own retention processes.

This website and external links

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have added no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, or cookies. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement linked below for details.

External links in the app, such as font source links, open in the system browser. When you visit GitHub, Google, or another external website, that service’s privacy practices also apply.

Players of different ages

The app does not actively collect personal data from players of any age. If a child provides personal information in a support email, a parent or guardian can contact the developer to request deletion.

Updates and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated date. If networking, analytics, or other data processing is added in the future, the policy and app privacy disclosures will be updated accordingly.

Developer and operator: Guan Jie Tan. For privacy or support questions, contact tripzy053@gmail.com.
