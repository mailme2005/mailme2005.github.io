How is my garden saved?

Your garden is saved on your current device when you finish editing and when the app leaves the foreground. Postcards have a separate in-game album. You can play offline without an account; cloud sync is not available.

Deleting the app may remove your local garden. Export postcards you want to keep to Photos first. A postcard is an image and cannot restore an editable garden.

How do I arrange things or undo?

In Arrange, choose a material and tap on grass to place it, or hold and drag to preview a position before releasing. A preview indicates whether the placement is valid.

In Adjust, select an object to rotate, resize, mirror, or copy it. Use Terrain brushes to draw paths, ponds, and grass with adjustable sizes. Paint grass along an edge to expand gradually, up to a 30 × 30 world-unit garden.

Undo and redo keep up to 50 steps during the current session. History is not kept after the app is closed and relaunched.

How do I name my bunny?

You can name your bunny from the companion card when you first meet, or skip it. Later, choose Rename bunny in Companion. Names can contain 1–12 characters. You can also call or pet your bunny and watch it wander, sniff flowers, or rest.

How do I switch languages?

Open the top-right menu or Garden notes, choose “中文 / English”, then select Chinese, English, or Follow system. Switching languages does not reset your garden or bunny name.

Why is the game silent?

Check your iPhone’s silent switch and volume, and the mute setting in Garden sounds. Music and nature sounds can be adjusted separately. After headphones disconnect, tap Resume playback in the sound panel.

How do I save or share a postcard?

Enter photo mode, capture and save a postcard, then choose Save to Photos or the system share button. On compact layouts, the camera may be inside the viewfinder menu.

Saving to Photos requests add-only permission the first time. You can change denied permission in iOS Settings. The in-game album works without Photos permission, and the app never reads your photo library.

What does starting a new garden remove?

Starting a new garden resets the current garden. The separate postcard album and your sound and language preferences are kept. If you only want to rearrange something, try Undo or Adjust first.

Questions about privacy or deletion?

Game records are stored on your device; the developer has no remote copy of your garden. Read the privacy policy for local data management, support-email deletion, and website access logs. You can email us if you need more help.
