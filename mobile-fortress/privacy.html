Privacy policy · 隐私政策

Scope / 适用范围

This policy applies to Mobile Fortress (移动堡垒) and this support website. The developer is Guan Jie Tan, publishing as Little Everyday Apps. Updated November 7, 2026.

本政策适用于《移动堡垒》和本支持网站。开发者为 Guan Jie Tan，发布名称为 Little Everyday Apps。更新日期：2026 年 11 月 7 日。

Offline play / 离线游玩

The game requires no account or internet connection. It does not collect or transmit personal information, identifiers, gameplay records or usage analytics to the developer. There are no ads, in-app purchases, tracking, third-party analytics, or requests for location, camera, microphone, contacts or photo access.

游戏无需账号或联网，不向开发者收集或传输个人信息、设备标识、游戏记录或使用统计。没有广告、内购、追踪、第三方分析工具，也不请求定位、相机、麦克风、通讯录或照片权限。

Local records / 本机记录

Parts, upgrades, route progress, best kills and audio settings are stored on your device. Banked parts remain after defeat or quitting. There is no game-provided cloud synchronization. Apple device backups may include local data according to your settings.

零件、升级、关卡进度、最佳击破数和声音设置保存在设备上，失败或退出后保留已入账零件。游戏不提供云同步；系统备份可能按你的设置保留本机数据。

Managing your data / 管理数据

The developer has no remote copy of your save. If the save cannot be read, you can export the original through the system share sheet, or explicitly start over after a local preservation copy is created. An exported copy goes only to the destination you choose. Recovery is not guaranteed. Delete the app and its data in iOS Settings to remove all local saves, preferences and preservation copies; Offload App retains documents and data. Manage device backups separately in your backup settings.

开发者没有存档的远程副本。存档无法读取时，可通过系统分享菜单导出原文件，或明确选择保留本机副本后重新开始。导出的副本只发送到你选择的目标；无法保证能修复。要删除全部本机存档、设置和保留副本，请在 iOS 设置中删除应用及其数据；“卸载 App”会保留文稿和数据。系统备份需在备份设置中另行管理。

Optional support email / 主动联系客服

If you email us, we receive your address, subject, message and any attachments you choose to send, solely to respond, troubleshoot or handle privacy requests. Do not send passwords or unnecessary personal information. Support uses Gmail, where Google processes email under its policies. Messages are retained only as needed to resolve and follow up on inquiries or meet applicable obligations, then deleted. Request access, correction or deletion from your original contact address; we may verify control of that address. Any legally required retention will be explained. Provider backups follow the provider's retention processes.

主动发送邮件时，我们会收到你的邮箱、主题、正文及你选择的附件，仅用于回复、排查或处理隐私请求。请勿发送密码或不必要的个人信息。客服使用 Gmail，Google 按其政策处理邮件。邮件仅在解决问题、跟进或履行适用义务所需期间保留，不再需要时删除。可从原联系邮箱请求查看、更正或删除；可能需要确认邮箱控制权。如依法需要保留，会说明原因。服务商备份遵循其自身保留机制。

Website and Apple services / 网站及系统服务

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages without added analytics, cookies, ad scripts or tracking pixels. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security. External sites have their own policies. App downloads, system diagnostics and backups are handled by Apple under its policies and your settings, separately from this game's own data practices.

本网站由 GitHub Pages 托管，未加入分析、Cookie、广告脚本或追踪像素。GitHub 可能为安全目的记录访问 IP。外部网站有各自政策。应用下载、系统诊断和备份由 Apple 按其政策和你的设置处理，与本游戏自身的数据处理分开。

Children and policy changes / 儿童与政策变更

The app does not actively collect personal data from players of any age. A parent or guardian may request deletion if a child sends personal information in a support email. If future features change data practices, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated.

应用不主动收集任何年龄玩家的个人数据。若儿童在客服邮件中提供个人信息，家长或监护人可请求删除。若未来功能改变数据处理方式，将更新本政策和商店隐私说明。
