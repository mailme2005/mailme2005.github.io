Support · 支持与帮助

How to play / 如何操作

Drag one finger anywhere on the battlefield to move; lift it to stop. The cannon always fires straight ahead automatically; move the vehicle to line up enemies. Circle along the road and keep an escape route. On the home screen, pick Campaign, Endless or Daily, then tap the start button at the bottom.

在战场内单指拖动移动，抬手停止。主炮始终朝前自动开火，通过走位让敌人进入火线；沿道路绕行并保留退路。在主页选择“战役”“无尽”或“每日”，再点底部的开始按钮。

Missions / 任务

Nine missions across three regions: Wasteland Road, Broken Bridge and Furnace Blockade. Each region has three mission types. Break the Blockade: survive six waves, then defeat the boss that arrives at 3:00. Elite Hunt: eliminate six gold-marked targets within 3 minutes. Destroy the Turrets: destroy three gun emplacements within 3 minutes; each one stops bombarding you when destroyed. Clear missions in order to unlock the next one. Choose any unlocked mission from Choose mission on the Campaign tab.

三个区域共九个任务：荒原公路、断桥防线、熔炉封锁线。每个区域有三种任务。突破封锁：撑过六波怪潮，击败 3:00 出现的 Boss。猎杀精英：3 分钟内消灭 6 个金色标记目标。摧毁炮台：3 分钟内摧毁 3 座炮台，摧毁后该炮台停止轰炸。按顺序通关解锁下一个任务，在“战役”标签的“选择关卡”中可选择任意已解锁任务。

Endless Breakout / 无尽突围

Unlocks after mission 3. The waves never end: every 4 regular waves bring a boss fight, then the next region. Enemies grow stronger each wave. Elites and a turret appear in later waves of each round for bonus points. Each wave offers a field upgrade. Kills pay half the campaign's alloy, plus bonuses for each wave and boss. Field support is available, but your record counts from the first time you were destroyed. With Game Center, your best score goes on the Endless leaderboard.

通过任务 3 后解锁。怪潮不会结束：每 4 波普通怪潮后迎来一场 Boss 战，之后进入下一个区域，敌人随波次越来越强。每轮后几波会出现精英和炮台，消灭可额外加分。每波都有一次改装机会。击杀合金为战役的一半，另有撑过每波和击败 Boss 的奖励。可以使用战地支援，但纪录按第一次被击毁时计算。登录 Game Center 后，最高分会进入无尽排行榜。

Daily Challenge / 每日挑战

Unlocks after mission 1. Each day (resetting at 00:00 UTC) everyone gets the same mission, cannon and daily modifier, on the same Lv.5 armor and cannon regardless of their own upgrades. Field support is off. Retry as often as you like; your result is your clear time. Your first clear each day pays 80 alloy, plus 10 for each consecutive day, up to 140. Missing a day resets the streak; your best streak is kept. With Game Center, your time goes on that day's leaderboard.

通过任务 1 后解锁。每天（UTC 0 点刷新）所有人同一个任务、同一门主炮和同一个当日词条，统一使用 Lv.5 装甲与主炮，与自己的升级无关，也不能使用战地支援。可以无限重试，成绩按完成用时计算。当天首次完成奖励 80 合金，连续完成每天多 10，最多 140；漏掉一天连续天数归零，最佳连续保留。登录 Game Center 后，用时会进入当天的排行榜。

Medals / 勋章

Each mission has three medals, 27 in total: Cleared for completing it, Bombproof for clearing it without taking artillery damage, and Swift Clear for finishing quickly (boss within 4:00, other missions within 2:00). Medals can be earned across separate runs.

每个任务有三枚勋章，共 27 枚：任务完成；炮火无伤（通关且未受炮击伤害）；迅速突破（4:00 内击败 Boss，其他任务 2:00 内完成）。可以分多次获得。

Warnings / 危险预警

Leave the marked artillery strip before the shells land. Dodge sideways from a charger's long red line. Leave the Boss's fan-shaped aim lines and red bombardment circles before the attack lands. Reinforcement warnings show where new enemies will enter. Warnings include visual shapes, captions and distinct sounds.

敌方炮击落地前离开标记带。冲锋怪长红线出现时横向躲开；Boss 扇形瞄准线及轰炸红圈出现后，在攻击落地前离开。援军预警提示怪物进入的位置。危险有图形、文字和独立音效提示。

Field upgrades / 战斗改装

Each run offers six free upgrade opportunities. Tap one of three icons at the lower right while combat continues. Each new opportunity grants one 1.2-second shield when you lift your control finger, or when you choose if already parked. Repeated lifts or selections do not refresh the shield, and shields do not accumulate. Resume dragging anywhere on the battlefield. Unused opportunities remain available until the run ends. When a base upgrade reaches the required level, an advanced mod (Penetrator Core, Aftershock or Mobile Reload) can appear as a green-star option. Field upgrades cost no alloy and last one run.

每局提供六次免费改装机会。点选右下方三个图标之一即可强化，战斗不会暂停。每个新机会提供一次 1.2 秒护盾：松开操控手指时触发，已停车则在点选时触发。重复松手或点选不会刷新，护盾次数不累积。选完可在战场任意位置继续拖动。未使用机会保留至本局结束。基础改装达到要求后，会出现绿色星标的进阶改装：贯穿弹芯、二次冲击、机动装填。战斗改装不消耗合金，仅当局生效。

Cannons / 主炮

The Autocannon fires rapidly at single targets. The Piercing Cannon passes through up to three enemies and unlocks after mission 1. The Blast Cannon fires slower, explosive shells and unlocks after mission 3. Switch cannons on the Fortress page before any run; permanent cannon upgrades apply to all three.

速射炮快速连发，压制单个目标。穿甲炮可穿过最多 3 个敌人，完成任务 1 后解锁。爆破炮射速较慢、命中会爆炸，完成任务 3 后解锁。出发前可在“战车”页面切换主炮；主炮永久升级对三门主炮都有效。

Alloy and upgrades / 合金与永久升级

Alloy is the game's only currency. Kills earn alloy, which is banked automatically, even on defeat. Clearing a mission adds a bonus, and the first clear of each mission adds an extra bonus once. Spend alloy on the Fortress page (open it from the home screen) on permanent armor and cannon upgrades, ten levels each. Levels 5 and 10 unlock extra effects: a launch shield and 10% less damage taken for armor; faster fire and +25% damage to bosses, elites and turrets for the cannon. Alloy also buys six cosmetic fortress paints; paints change only how your fortress looks and can be switched freely once bought. Alloy is earned only by playing and cannot be bought.

合金是游戏中唯一的货币。击杀获得合金并自动入账，失败也保留；通关有奖励，每个任务首次通关另有一次额外奖励。在“战车”页面（从主页进入）用合金永久升级装甲和主炮，各十级。5 级和 10 级会解锁额外效果：装甲为开局护盾、所受伤害 -10%；主炮为射速提升、对 Boss、精英和炮台伤害 +25%。合金还可以购买 6 套战车涂装，涂装只改变外观，买下后可随时免费切换。合金只能通过游戏获得，不能购买。

Field support / 战地支援

When your health falls below 30% and you have enough alloy, an Emergency repair button appears at the lower right of the battlefield: 40 alloy restores 40% health with a 2-second shield, once per run. If your vehicle is destroyed, you can spend alloy to restart on the spot (60 the first time, 120 the second, at most twice per run) with half health, a 3-second shield and a shock wave that clears nearby regular enemies. Alloy earned in the run is kept. Running out of time cannot be restarted. Both are optional: every mission can be cleared without them, and choosing End run settles the run as usual. Field support is not available in the Daily Challenge.

耐久低于 30% 且合金足够时，战场右下角会出现“紧急维修”：花 40 合金恢复 40% 耐久并获得 2 秒护盾，每局 1 次。战车被击毁时，可花合金原地重启（第一次 60、第二次 120，每局最多 2 次），恢复一半耐久并获得 3 秒护盾，冲击波会震开周围的普通敌人，本局已获得的合金不受影响。任务超时不能重启。两者都是可选的，不使用也能通关；选择结束本局会照常结算。每日挑战中不能使用战地支援。

Game Center

Game Center is optional. If your device is signed in, the game reports 36 achievements (including all 27 medals) and two leaderboards: Endless Breakout best score and Daily Challenge time. Medals you earned before signing in are reported automatically. Tap the Game Center button at the top of the home screen to see your status and open achievements or leaderboards. If you are not signed in, everything else works the same; sign in under Settings › Game Center on your device.

Game Center 是可选的。设备登录后，游戏会上报 36 个成就（含全部 27 枚勋章）和两个排行榜：无尽突围最高分、每日挑战用时。登录前已获得的勋章会自动补报。点主页顶部的 Game Center 按钮可查看登录状态、打开成就或排行榜。没有登录时其他功能完全一样；可在设备“设置 › Game Center”中登录。

Language / 语言

Open Game settings (the gear at the top right of the home screen) to choose English or Simplified Chinese. You can also change language in the pause menu; your choice is saved on this device. On first launch the game follows your device language.

打开游戏设置（主页右上角的齿轮）选择英文或简体中文，也可在暂停菜单中切换，选择会保存在本机。首次打开时跟随设备语言。

No sound / 没有声音

Check your device's silent mode and system volume, then open Game settings on the home screen. Music and effects have independent sliders; 0% mutes that category. Returning from background, an interruption or disconnected headphones may require tapping Resume or starting a new run.

检查设备静音开关和系统音量，再打开主页的游戏设置。音乐与音效独立调节，0% 为该项静音。切回前台、音频中断或拔掉耳机后，可能需要点击继续或开始新的一局。

Save issues / 存档问题

Do not delete the app while troubleshooting. Free storage if saving fails and tap Retry save. If the save is damaged, export the original before choosing to start over; a local copy is preserved. There is no cloud save or remote recovery. Quitting a run banks earned alloy but does not resume combat at the same position. Updating the app keeps your progress.

排查期间请勿删除应用。保存失败时释放空间并点击重试；损坏存档可先导出，再选择保留副本后重开。没有云存档或远程恢复。退出一局保留已入账合金，但不会从同一战斗位置继续。更新应用会保留进度。
