Support · 支持与帮助

How to play / 如何操作

Drag one finger anywhere on the battlefield to move; lift it to stop. The cannon always fires straight ahead automatically; move the vehicle to line up enemies. Circle along the road and keep an escape route. Reach the Boss after 180 seconds and defeat it to clear the route.

在战场内单指拖动移动，抬手停止。主炮始终朝前自动开火，通过走位让敌人进入火线；沿道路绕行并保留退路。坚持 180 秒抵达 Boss，击败它即可通关。

Warnings / 危险预警

Leave the marked artillery strip before the shells land. Dodge sideways from a charger's long red line. Leave the Boss's fan-shaped aim lines and red bombardment circles before the attack lands. Reinforcement warnings show where new enemies will enter. Warnings include visual shapes, captions and distinct sounds.

敌方炮击落地前离开标记带。冲锋怪长红线出现时横向躲开；Boss 扇形瞄准线及轰炸红圈出现后，在攻击落地前离开。援军预警提示怪物进入的位置。危险有图形、文字和独立音效提示。

Upgrades and routes / 改装与关卡

Each run offers six free upgrade opportunities. Tap one of three icons at the lower right while combat continues. Each new opportunity grants one 1.2-second shield when you lift your control finger, or when you choose if already parked. Repeated lifts or selections do not refresh the shield, and shields do not accumulate. Resume dragging anywhere on the battlefield. Unused opportunities remain available until the run ends. Field upgrades last one run. Parts from kills are banked automatically, even on defeat, and buy permanent armor and cannon upgrades in the garage. Each has ten levels. Clear routes in order to unlock all three; replaying still earns parts, but first-clear bonuses are awarded once per route.

每局提供六次免费改装机会。点选右下方三个图标之一即可强化，战斗不会暂停。每个新机会提供一次 1.2 秒护盾：松开操控手指时触发，已停车则在点选时触发。重复松手或点选不会刷新，护盾次数不累积。选完可在战场任意位置继续拖动。未使用机会保留至本局结束，改装仅当局生效。击杀零件自动入账，失败也保留，可在车库永久升级战车和主炮，各十级。顺序通关解锁三条路线；重玩仍有零件，首通奖励每关只有一次。

Language / 语言

Tap 中文 / English in the garage to choose English or Simplified Chinese. You can also change language in Game settings or the pause menu; your choice is saved on this device.

点击首页的“中文 / English”选择英文或简体中文，也可在游戏设置或暂停菜单中切换，选择会保存在本机。

No sound / 没有声音

Check your device's silent mode and system volume, then open the garage game settings. Music and effects have independent sliders; 0% mutes that category. Returning from background, an interruption or disconnected headphones may require tapping Resume or starting a new run.

检查设备静音开关和系统音量，再打开车库游戏设置。音乐与音效独立调节，0% 为该项静音。切回前台、音频中断或拔掉耳机后，可能需要点击继续或开始新的一局。

Save issues / 存档问题

Do not delete the app while troubleshooting. Free storage if saving fails and tap Retry save. If the save is damaged, export the original before choosing to start over; a local copy is preserved. There is no cloud save or remote recovery. Quitting a run banks earned parts but does not resume combat at the same position.

排查期间请勿删除应用。保存失败时释放空间并点击重试；损坏存档可先导出，再选择保留副本后重开。没有云存档或远程恢复。退出一局保留已入账零件，但不会从同一战斗位置继续。
