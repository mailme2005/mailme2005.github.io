Support · 支持与帮助

How to play / 如何操作

Drag one finger anywhere on the battlefield to move; lift it to stop. The cannon targets the closest enemy automatically. Circle along the road and keep an escape route. Reach the Boss after 180 seconds and defeat it to clear the route.

在战场内单指拖动移动，抬手停止。主炮自动瞄准最近敌人；沿道路绕行并保留退路。坚持 180 秒抵达 Boss，击败它即可通关。

Warnings / 危险预警

Dodge sideways from a charger's long red line. Leave the Boss's fan-shaped aim lines and red bombardment circles before the attack lands. Reinforcement warnings show where new enemies will enter. Warnings include visual shapes, captions and distinct sounds.

冲锋怪长红线出现时横向躲开；Boss 扇形瞄准线及轰炸红圈出现后，在攻击落地前离开。援军预警提示怪物进入的位置。危险有图形、文字和独立音效提示。

Upgrades and routes / 改装与关卡

Each run offers six free field upgrades, with combat paused while you choose. These last one run. Parts from kills are banked automatically, even on defeat, and buy permanent armor and cannon upgrades in the garage. Each has ten levels. Clear routes in order to unlock all three; replaying still earns parts, but first-clear bonuses are awarded once per route.

每局提供六次免费战斗改装，选择时暂停，仅当局生效。击杀零件自动入账，失败也保留，可在车库永久升级战车和主炮，各十级。顺序通关解锁三条路线；重玩仍有零件，首通奖励每关只有一次。

No sound / 没有声音

Check your device's silent mode and system volume, then open the garage audio settings. Music and effects have independent sliders; 0% mutes that category. Returning from background, an interruption or disconnected headphones may require tapping Resume or starting a new run.

检查设备静音开关和系统音量，再打开车库声音设置。音乐与音效独立调节，0% 为该项静音。切回前台、音频中断或拔掉耳机后，可能需要点击继续或开始新的一局。

Save issues / 存档问题

Do not delete the app while troubleshooting. Free storage if saving fails and tap Retry save. If the save is damaged, export the original before choosing to start over; a local copy is preserved. There is no cloud save or remote recovery. Quitting a run banks earned parts but does not resume combat at the same position.

排查期间请勿删除应用。保存失败时释放空间并点击重试；损坏存档可先导出，再选择保留副本后重开。没有云存档或远程恢复。退出一局保留已入账零件，但不会从同一战斗位置继续。
