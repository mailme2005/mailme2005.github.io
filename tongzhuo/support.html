From the table to everyone’s share / 从点餐到每人金额

There are three steps. Order: note who had what, by typing or with your keyboard’s dictation, while it is still fresh. Receipt: photograph the receipt or enter it by hand, then check the items, amounts, currency, tax, service charge, tip and any discount. Split: review each person’s share and confirm. You can start with the receipt instead, and you can always tap an item to assign it by hand.

一共三步。点餐：趁还记得，打字或用键盘听写，记下谁点了什么。收据：拍下收据或手动输入，再核对菜品、金额、币种、税费、服务费、小费和折扣。分账：查看每个人的金额并确认。也可以先从收据开始，任何时候都可以点一道菜手动分配。

What is free? / 哪些功能免费？

Entering receipts, on-device receipt reading, splitting by hand, saved bills, backups and sharing are free and need no account. AI help is optional. Tongzhuo never moves money and does not collect payments from your friends.

手动输入收据、本机识别收据、手动分账、历史账单、备份和分享都是免费的，也不需要账号。AI 协助是可选的。同桌不会转账，也不会替你向朋友收款。

How do AI splits count? / AI 次数怎么算？

One AI split covers one whole dinner. Organizing the order, reading the receipt photo and suggesting the split all count as the same one, for 24 hours from the first AI request for that dinner. Each dinner allows up to 8 organizing or splitting requests and 2 receipt photo readings; after that you can still finish by hand. Each App Store account gets 3 free AI splits, with no sign-in, and you can buy a pack of 10. Purchased AI splits do not expire. There is no subscription and nothing renews automatically. Open AI splits to see your balance and what the current dinner has left.

一次 AI 额度管一顿饭。整理点餐、识别收据照片、生成分账建议都算在同一次里，从这顿饭第一次请求 AI 起 24 小时内有效。每顿饭最多 8 次整理或分账请求、2 次收据照片识别，用完后仍可手动完成。每个 App Store 账号赠送 3 次，不需要登录，也可以购买 10 次包。已购买的次数不会过期，没有订阅，也不会自动续费。打开“AI 分账次数”可以查看余额，以及这顿饭还剩多少次。

What if a request fails? / 请求失败怎么办？

If a request could not be sent, or the AI service refused it, nothing is deducted. If a dinner’s very first request fails without any usable result, its AI split is returned (a limited number of times per account). If the connection drops while waiting, do not start over: choose Resume previous request on the same bill and the saved answer is fetched without another charge. Tongzhuo never retries a paid request by itself.

请求没能发出，或被 AI 服务拒绝时，不会扣次。一顿饭的第一个请求失败且没有任何可用结果时，这次额度会退回（每个账号有次数限制）。等待时连接中断，请不要重新开始：在同一张账单上点“继续上次处理”，即可取回已保存的结果，不会再扣一次。同桌不会自行重试付费请求。

Currencies, tips and discounts / 币种、小费与折扣

Choose the bill’s currency when you enter the receipt; 73 currencies are supported, including ones without decimals such as yen and won. Amounts can be typed with a decimal point or a decimal comma. Changing the currency never converts amounts. Tax, service charge, tip and a whole-bill discount are shared in proportion to what each person ate; for a discount on one dish, change that dish’s amount instead.

输入收据时选择账单的币种，支持 73 个币种，包括日元、韩元这类没有小数的。金额可以用小数点或小数逗号输入。更换币种不会换算金额。税费、服务费、小费和整单折扣按每个人的餐费比例分摊；只属于某道菜的折扣，请直接改那道菜的金额。

Receipt reading and accuracy / 收据识别与准确度

On-device reading is free and works best with printed English or Chinese receipts that show prices with two decimals. AI reading handles more languages and layouts. Either way the result is a draft: check it against the photo, and the app will not save a receipt whose items and charges do not add up to the total you confirm. AI suggestions can be wrong; review each one before confirming.

本机识别免费，最适合印刷清晰、金额带两位小数的英文或中文收据。AI 识别能处理更多语言和版式。无论哪种方式，结果都只是草稿：请对照照片核对，项目与费用加起来不等于你确认的总额时，App 不会保存收据。AI 的建议可能出错，确认前请逐项检查。

New device or missing purchase? / 换手机或购买未到账？

Use the same App Store account on the new device, then open AI splits and choose Refresh balance & check purchases (or Show my AI splits if no balance is shown yet). There is nothing to sign in to inside Tongzhuo. The AI split balance is kept by our service, separately from your App Store purchase history. Bills themselves stay on the device where you saved them and do not sync; use Backup & restore to move them. Do not delete the app while looking for a missing bill.

在新设备的 App Store 使用同一个账号，再打开“AI 分账次数”点“刷新余额并检查未完成购买”（还没有显示余额时点“查看我的 AI 次数”）。同桌里不需要登录。AI 分账余额由我们的服务保存，与 App Store 购买记录分开。账单本身只保存在原设备上，不会同步；可以用“备份与恢复”转移。排查账单丢失时请不要删除 App。

Language / 语言

Choose 中文 or English at the top of the home screen. Dish names and people’s names are kept exactly as you enter them.

在首页上方选择“中文”或“English”。菜名和人名会按你输入的原样保留。

Refunds and deletion / 退款与删除

Apple handles refunds for purchases at Report a Problem. A refunded pack is removed from your balance. To delete your online AI records, open AI splits → Delete my online AI records; unused AI splits are forfeited, using AI again later does not include free splits, and no refund is requested automatically. Bills saved on your device are separate: delete one in Saved bills by swiping left on it, or delete the app to remove them all.

内购退款由 Apple 处理，可在上述链接申请。已退款的次数包会从余额中扣除。删除线上的 AI 使用记录请打开“AI 分账次数 → 删除线上的 AI 使用记录”；未使用的次数作废，以后再次使用 AI 时不再赠送免费次数，也不会自动申请退款。保存在设备上的账单是分开的：在“历史账单”里向左滑可以删除某一张，删除 App 则全部移除。
