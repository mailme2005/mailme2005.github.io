Before launch / 上线准备中

Tongzhuo is currently in private testing. Public online AI and in-app purchases are not available yet. The AI package described below is the planned launch offering; the app will show the available service and local price before any purchase.

同桌目前处于私人测试阶段，线上 AI 和内购尚未向公众开放。下方为计划上线的次数包；是否可用及当地价格，以购买前 App 显示为准。

From receipt to everyone’s share / 从收据到每人金额

Scan one complete receipt, then check the items, amounts, tax and service charge. Add the people at the table and choose who is speaking. Say who is covering each item, review the transcript, and request an AI suggestion. Answer any questions, then confirm the split. You can also tap any item to assign its cost by hand.

扫描一张完整收据，核对菜品、金额、税及服务费。加入参与者并选定说话的人，说明谁承担哪些菜品，检查转写文字后请求 AI 建议。回答不确定的问题，最后确认分配。也可以点选菜品，直接手动分配费用。

What stays free? / 哪些功能免费？

Receipt scanning, manual splitting, saved bills and sharing totals are free. Optional OpenAI transcription requires internet access and a separate transcription allowance. AI suggestions are optional. Tongzhuo does not move money or collect payments from your friends.

收据扫描、手动分账、历史账单和分享金额均免费。可选 OpenAI 转写需要联网，并使用独立语音额度。AI 建议是可选功能。同桌不会转账，也不会代你向朋友收款。

How will AI splits work? / AI 次数如何计算？

The launch plan includes 2 free AI splits on first sign-in and an optional pack of 10. Each split includes one initial suggestion and up to 4 follow-up answers within 24 hours. Changing the receipt, people, confirmed assignments or original instructions starts a new split. Manual corrections never use AI splits. Purchased splits do not expire. There is no subscription or automatic renewal.

计划首次登录赠送 2 次 AI 分账，可另外购买 10 次包。每次包含首次建议和最多 4 轮补充回答，须在 24 小时内完成。修改收据、参与者、已确认分配或原始说明，会开始新的一次。手动修改不消耗 AI 次数，已购买次数不会过期，没有订阅或自动续费。

What if the connection fails? / 网络中断怎么办？

Open AI splits → Check last AI request. Do not repeatedly start new requests. A technical failure returns the reserved split; an interrupted connection may still have a saved result. Submit the same instructions on the same bill to retrieve it without using another split. A successfully generated suggestion uses a split even if you decide not to apply it. You can always continue by hand.

打开“AI 分账次数 → 检查上次 AI 请求状态”，不要重复发起新请求。技术失败会退回预留次数；连接中断时，服务器可能已经保存结果。回到同一账单提交相同说明，可取回结果而不重复扣次。成功生成建议后，即使不采用也会使用一次；随时可以改用手动分账。

New device or missing purchase? / 换手机或购买未到账？

For online AI, sign in with the same Apple account used in Tongzhuo, then choose Refresh balance & check purchases. The server balance is separate from the App Store’s purchase history. Bills themselves stay on the device where you saved them and do not sync between devices. Do not delete the app while troubleshooting a missing bill.

线上 AI 请使用原来登录同桌的 Apple 账号，再点“刷新余额并检查未完成购买”。服务器余额与 App Store 购买记录分开管理。历史账单只在原设备保存，不会跨设备同步。排查账单丢失时请勿删除 App。

Language, speech and accuracy / 语言、语音与准确度

Choose 中文 / English at the top of the home screen. The interface is bilingual. With your permission, recordings of up to 55 seconds are sent to OpenAI when you finish speaking; language is detected automatically. Review the transcript before asking Claude to suggest a split. Say names and item names clearly, pause between instructions, and check the text. AI may misunderstand; review every suggestion before confirming.

首页上方可选择“中文 / English”。经你同意后，最长 55 秒的录音会在结束时发送至 OpenAI，自动识别语言。核对文字后再交给 Claude 生成分账建议。请说清人名和菜名，在指令间停顿，并核对文字。AI 可能理解错误，确认前请检查每项建议。

Refunds and deletion / 退款与删除

Apple handles purchase refunds at Report a Problem. Refunded packs are removed from your balance. To delete your online account, open AI splits → Delete online account and verify with Apple. This forfeits unused splits and does not automatically request a refund. Delete saved bills separately in Saved bills.

内购退款由 Apple 处理，可访问上述链接申请。退款次数包会从余额中扣除。删除线上账号请打开“AI 分账次数 → 删除线上账号”并通过 Apple 验证；未用次数将作废，不会自动申请退款。本机账单需在历史记录中另外删除。
