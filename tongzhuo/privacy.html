Privacy policy
隐私政策

This policy covers Tongzhuo (同桌) and these support pages. The developer is Guan Jie Tan, publishing as Little Everyday Apps. Updated November 11, 2026.

本政策适用于同桌及本支持网站。开发者为 Guan Jie Tan，发布名称为 Little Everyday Apps。更新日期：2026 年 11 月 11 日。

Current availability / 当前状态

Public online AI, accounts and purchases are not yet enabled. The sections about those features describe their intended release implementation. The private test build uses a paired Mac to forward approved text requests to Claude; its local test ledger stores request hashes, usage and results. Testers can ask the developer to remove that test data.

公众线上 AI、账号及内购尚未启用；相关条款描述计划发布版本的实现。私人测试版通过配对 Mac 将获准提交的文字转发给 Claude，本机测试账本保存请求摘要、用量和结果。测试者可联系开发者删除测试数据。

On your device / 留在设备上的内容

Receipt recognition and speech transcription run on your device. Tongzhuo does not upload receipt photographs or audio recordings and does not keep audio files. It saves bill details, participant names or nicknames, confirmed splits, typed instructions and bill history locally. These app-managed bill files are excluded from iCloud device backup. We do not provide cloud bill sync or remote bill recovery. Content you export goes to the destination you choose.

收据识别和语音转写在本机执行，不上传收据照片或录音，也不保存录音文件。菜品、金额、参与者姓名或昵称、已确认分配、输入文字及历史账单保存在本机；这些账单文件不加入 iCloud 设备备份。应用不提供云端账单同步或远程恢复。导出的内容只发送到你选择的目标。

Optional AI / 可选 AI

Before your first AI request, the app asks for permission to send reviewed items and amounts, participant nicknames and aliases, who is speaking, confirmed allocations, your instructions and follow-up answers through our server to Anthropic’s Claude API. This is used to suggest allocations and ask for clarification. Use nicknames and avoid unnecessary sensitive information. You can withdraw permission under AI splits; this stops future AI submissions and does not recall requests already sent. Manual splitting remains available.

首次 AI 请求前，App 会征求同意，将核对后的菜品和金额、参与者昵称及别名、说话者、已确认分配、分账文字和追问回答，经服务器发送到 Anthropic 的 Claude API，用于生成分配建议及澄清问题。建议使用昵称，避免不必要的敏感资料。可在“AI 分账次数”中撤回授权，停止之后的 AI 提交；无法撤回已经发出的请求，手动分账仍可使用。

Anthropic describes standard API input/output retention of up to 30 days, with exceptions for legal obligations, safety enforcement or a separate agreement. We have not claimed a zero-retention agreement. See Anthropic’s API retention policy and API data practices.

Anthropic 说明，标准 API 输入及输出通常在 30 天内删除，法律、安全执行或另行协议等情况除外。我们没有宣称已获零保留协议。详情见上述服务商政策。

Accounts and service security / 账号与服务安全

Online AI uses Sign in with Apple and Firebase Authentication. We do not request your name or email for sign-in. Apple and Firebase process the identifiers and tokens required to authenticate you. Firebase App Check with Apple App Attest verifies the app to help prevent abuse. Google Cloud hosts the service and server ledger. Providers may process IP addresses, device/app information, security events and service logs under their policies. The service may involve processing outside your country.

线上 AI 使用 Apple 登录和 Firebase Authentication，不为登录索取姓名或邮箱。Apple 和 Firebase 处理身份验证所需标识及令牌；Firebase App Check 与 Apple App Attest 验证应用，帮助防止滥用。Google Cloud 托管服务及服务器账本。服务商可能依据其政策处理 IP、设备或应用信息、安全事件和服务日志，处理地点可能在你所在国家或地区之外。

Balances, purchases and request records / 余额、购买与请求记录

We keep a pseudonymous account reference, free and purchased balances, an app account token and verified transaction identifiers to deliver purchases, prevent duplicate credit, process refunds and enforce usage limits. Apple processes payments; we do not receive payment card numbers. Request hashes, generated allocation results and clarification questions are kept to recover interrupted requests and prevent duplicate charges. The service does not save full receipt input or original transcripts in its request ledger. Request results and expired clarification state are scheduled for removal after 7 days, with up to 24 hours for the daily cleanup. Balance and transaction records remain as needed to provide purchased usage and prevent replay.

我们保存去标识化账号引用、免费及付费余额、应用账号令牌和经验证的交易标识，以发放次数、防止重复入账、处理退款及限制滥用。付款由 Apple 处理，我们不会收到银行卡号。请求摘要、生成的分配结果及追问问题用于恢复中断请求和避免重复扣次；服务器请求账本不保存完整收据输入或原始转写。请求结果及过期追问状态计划在 7 天后清理，日常清理最多另需 24 小时。余额和交易记录在提供已购买用量及防止重复交易所需期间保留。

Your controls and deletion / 管理与删除

Delete individual bills in Saved bills. Delete the app and its data in iOS Settings to remove local records and preferences; Offload App retains data. For an online account, choose AI splits → Delete online account and verify your identity with Apple. Online request content and the usable balance are deleted; unused splits are forfeited. Local bills are separate and remain until you delete them. Minimal pseudonymous trial-claim and transaction records are retained to prevent repeat free claims and purchase replay. Provider security logs and backups follow their retention policies. Email us for access, correction or deletion requests; we may verify your identity and explain any required retention.

可在历史记录中逐笔删除账单，或在 iOS 设置中删除 App 及其数据，移除本机记录和设置；“卸载 App”会保留数据。删除线上账号请在“AI 分账次数 → 删除线上账号”再次通过 Apple 验证。线上请求内容及可用余额会删除，未用次数作废；本机账单需另外删除。为防止重复领取免费次数及重放交易，会保留最少量的去标识化领取与交易记录。服务商安全日志和备份遵循其自身政策。可通过邮箱申请查看、更正或删除资料，可能需要验证身份，并会说明必须保留的部分。

No advertising or tracking / 无广告及追踪

Tongzhuo has no ads, cross-app tracking or third-party analytics SDK. We do not sell your personal information. The app does not request contacts or location access. Camera and microphone access are optional and used only when you choose the corresponding feature.

同桌没有广告、跨应用追踪或第三方分析 SDK，不出售个人信息，不请求通讯录或定位权限。相机及麦克风权限为可选，仅在你选择对应功能时使用。

Support and this website / 客服及网站

If you email us, we receive your address, message and chosen attachments to respond and resolve your request. Support uses Gmail. Messages are kept only as needed for support, follow-up or applicable obligations, then deleted. These pages use GitHub Pages without added analytics, cookies or tracking scripts. GitHub may log IP addresses for security. Apple handles app distribution, system diagnostics and purchases under its policies and your settings.

主动发送客服邮件时，我们会收到邮箱、正文和你选择的附件，仅用于回复及解决问题。客服使用 Gmail，邮件在支持、跟进或适用义务所需期间保留，不再需要后删除。网站使用 GitHub Pages，未加入分析、Cookie 或追踪脚本；GitHub 可能为安全目的记录 IP。应用分发、系统诊断及内购由 Apple 按其政策和你的设置处理。
