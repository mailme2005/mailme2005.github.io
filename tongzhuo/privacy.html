Privacy policy
隐私政策

This policy covers Tongzhuo (同桌) and these support pages. The developer is Guan Jie Tan, publishing as Little Everyday Apps. Updated November 18, 2026.

本政策适用于同桌及本支持网站。开发者为 Guan Jie Tan，发布名称为 Little Everyday Apps。更新日期：2026 年 11 月 18 日。

Before launch / 上线准备中

Tongzhuo is not on the App Store yet. This page describes the version being prepared for release; sign-in, AI splits and purchases become available when it launches.

同桌尚未在 App Store 上架。本页描述的是准备发布的版本，登录、AI 分账次数和购买在上架后才可使用。

In short / 概要

Tongzhuo works without an account, and your bills stay on your device. Online AI is optional: only when you sign in and give permission does the app send what you typed, the bill details or a receipt photo to our service and on to Anthropic’s Claude. There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics SDK.

同桌不需要账号即可使用，账单保存在你的设备上。线上 AI 是可选功能：只有在你登录并同意之后，App 才会把你输入的文字、账单内容或收据照片发送到我们的服务，再转给 Anthropic 的 Claude。没有广告、没有追踪、没有分析 SDK。

On your device / 留在设备上的内容

Tongzhuo saves your bills on the device: items, amounts, currency, tip and discount, the names or nicknames of the people at the table, what each person ordered, confirmed splits and the text you typed. It also keeps up to three local recovery copies, and records of unfinished AI requests (including a suggestion that has already come back) so interrupted work can be resumed. Nothing changes an amount until you review and confirm it. While you are reviewing a receipt, its photo and your draft are kept in protected local files; the photo is removed once the reviewed receipt is saved, and an unfinished draft stays until you finish or delete it. These files are excluded from iCloud device backup. Tongzhuo has no cloud bill sync and cannot recover bills remotely. Anything you export or share goes only to the destination you choose. On-device receipt reading uses Apple’s text recognition on your device; the photo is not uploaded. If you dictate with the keyboard’s microphone key, the dictation is handled by the keyboard you use under its own terms; Tongzhuo only receives the resulting text.

同桌把账单保存在设备上：菜品、金额、币种、小费与折扣、同桌人的姓名或昵称、各人点了什么、已确认的分配以及你输入的文字。应用还保留最近三份本机恢复备份，以及未完成的 AI 请求记录（包括已经返回的建议），用于恢复被打断的操作。任何金额都要经你核对确认后才会改变。核对收据期间，照片和草稿保存在受系统保护的本机文件里；核对后的收据保存成功即清理照片，未完成的草稿保留到你完成或删除为止。这些文件不加入 iCloud 设备备份。同桌没有云端账单同步，也无法远程恢复账单。你导出或分享的内容只会发送到你选择的目标。本机收据识别使用设备上的 Apple 文字识别，不上传照片。如果你用键盘上的麦克风键听写，听写由你所用的键盘按其自身条款处理，同桌只收到转成的文字。

Optional AI for orders and splits / 可选的 AI 整理点餐与分账

Before the first AI request, the app asks for permission. After you agree, it sends the following through our service (hosted on Google Cloud in Singapore) to Anthropic’s Claude API: the text you typed and your answers to follow-up questions; dish names; the nicknames and aliases of the people at the table and who is speaking; and, once a receipt is attached, the reviewed items, amounts, currency, tip, discount and the splits you have already confirmed. This is used only to organize the order, suggest a split and ask about anything unclear. Please use nicknames and leave out anything sensitive that is not needed. You can withdraw permission under AI splits; this stops further requests but cannot recall one already sent. Everything can still be done by hand.

第一次请求 AI 之前，App 会先征求同意。同意后，以下内容会经我们的服务（托管在 Google Cloud 新加坡区域）发送到 Anthropic 的 Claude API：你输入的文字和对追问的回答；菜名；同桌人的昵称与别名，以及说话的人是谁；添加收据之后，还包括核对过的菜品、金额、币种、小费、折扣和你已确认的分配。这些内容只用于整理点餐、生成分账建议和就不清楚的地方提问。建议使用昵称，不要填写不需要的敏感信息。可在“AI 分账次数”里撤回同意，之后不再发送新请求，但已经发出的请求无法撤回。所有操作都仍然可以手动完成。

Optional AI receipt reading / 可选的 AI 收据识别

With a separate permission, a receipt photo can be read by Claude instead of on the device. The app first resizes the photo and removes its metadata (such as location), then sends it through our service to Anthropic’s Claude API. Our service does not save the photo. It does save the extracted items, amounts, uncertainty flags, a hash of the request and usage figures, so a result can be recovered after a lost connection without a second model call. Cover personal or payment details you do not need before taking the photo. You can withdraw this permission under AI splits.

经你另行同意，收据照片可以交给 Claude 识别，而不是在本机识别。App 会先缩小照片并移除其中的元数据（例如位置），再经我们的服务发送到 Anthropic 的 Claude API。我们的服务不保存照片，但会保存提取出的菜品、金额、不确定标记、请求摘要和用量，以便在连接中断后取回结果，而不用再次调用模型。拍照前请遮住不需要的个人或付款信息。可在“AI 分账次数”里撤回这项同意。

How Anthropic handles this data / Anthropic 如何处理这些数据

Anthropic describes standard API input and output retention of up to 30 days, with exceptions for legal obligations, safety enforcement or a separate agreement. We have not claimed a zero-retention agreement. See Anthropic’s API retention policy and API data practices. We do not send your account identifier to Anthropic.

Anthropic 说明，标准 API 的输入和输出通常在 30 天内删除，法律义务、安全执行或另有协议的情况除外。我们没有宣称签有零保留协议。详情见上述服务商政策。我们不会把你的账号标识发送给 Anthropic。

Accounts and service security / 账号与服务安全

Online AI uses Sign in with Apple and Firebase Authentication. We do not ask for your name or email address to sign in. Apple and Firebase process the identifiers and tokens needed to authenticate you. Firebase App Check with Apple App Attest verifies that requests come from the genuine app, to help prevent abuse. Google Cloud hosts the service and its records. These providers may process IP addresses, device or app information, security events and service logs under their own policies, and processing may take place outside your country.

线上 AI 使用 Apple 登录和 Firebase Authentication，登录不索取姓名或邮箱。Apple 和 Firebase 处理身份验证所需的标识和令牌；Firebase App Check 与 Apple App Attest 验证请求来自正版应用，帮助防止滥用。Google Cloud 托管服务及其记录。这些服务商可能依据各自的政策处理 IP 地址、设备或应用信息、安全事件和服务日志，处理地点可能在你所在国家或地区之外。

Balances, purchases and request records / 余额、购买与请求记录

We keep a pseudonymous account reference, your free and purchased AI split balances, an app account token and verified transaction identifiers, in order to deliver purchases, prevent duplicate credit, handle refunds and enforce usage limits. Apple processes payments; we never receive card numbers. For each dinner that uses AI we keep counters (how many requests it has used and when its 24 hours end). For each request we keep a hash, its status, and the returned suggestion or extracted receipt details, so an interrupted request can be recovered and is not charged twice. Request results and expired dinner records are removed about 7 days later, with up to 24 more hours for the daily cleanup. Balance and transaction records are kept for as long as needed to honor purchased use and prevent replay.

我们保存去标识化的账号引用、免费与已购买的 AI 分账余额、应用账号令牌和经验证的交易标识，用于发放次数、防止重复入账、处理退款和执行用量限制。付款由 Apple 处理，我们不会收到银行卡号。每顿使用过 AI 的饭，我们保存计数（已用了几次请求、24 小时何时结束）。每个请求保存摘要、状态，以及返回的建议或提取出的收据明细，用于恢复被打断的请求并避免重复扣次。请求结果和过期的聚餐记录约 7 天后清理，日常清理最多另需 24 小时。余额和交易记录在兑现已购买用量及防止重复交易所需的期间内保留。

Your controls and deletion / 管理与删除

Saved bills live only on your device. To delete one, open Saved bills, swipe left on it and choose Delete; it is also removed from the recovery copies on the device, along with any unfinished receipt draft for it. Backup files you exported yourself are not changed. To remove everything, delete the app and its data in iOS Settings; Offload App keeps the data. To delete your online account, open AI splits → Delete online account and confirm with Apple. Online request content and your usable balance are deleted and unused AI splits are forfeited; bills on your device are separate and stay until you remove them. A minimal pseudonymous record of the free-trial claim and of transactions is kept to prevent repeat claims and purchase replay. Provider security logs and backups follow the providers’ own retention. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Email us to make a request; we may need to verify your identity and will explain anything we must keep.

已保存的账单只在你的设备上。要删除某一张，请打开“历史账单”，在它上面向左滑并选择删除；它也会从设备上的恢复备份中移除，连同它未完成的收据草稿。你自己导出的备份文件不会被改动。要全部移除，请在 iOS 设置中删除 App 及其数据；“卸载 App”会保留数据。删除线上账号请打开“AI 分账次数 → 删除线上账号”并通过 Apple 确认。线上请求内容和可用余额会被删除，未使用的 AI 分账次数作废；设备上的账单是分开的，会保留到你自行移除。为防止重复领取免费次数和重放交易，会保留最少量的去标识化领取与交易记录。服务商的安全日志和备份遵循其自身的保留政策。视你所在地区的法律，你可能有权要求查阅、更正或删除你的资料，反对或限制其使用，并向当地数据保护机构投诉。可通过邮箱提出请求，我们可能需要核实身份，并会说明必须保留的部分。

Why we process this data / 处理依据

We process AI request content on the basis of the permission you give in the app, which you can withdraw at any time. We process account, balance and purchase records to provide the AI splits you obtained, and limited security and anti-abuse records because we have a legitimate interest in keeping the service safe and fair. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling and we do not sell it.

AI 请求内容的处理以你在 App 内给出的同意为依据，你可以随时撤回。账号、余额和购买记录的处理是为了向你提供已获得的 AI 分账次数；有限的安全与防滥用记录，是基于保障服务安全和公平的正当利益。我们不会把你的资料用于广告或画像，也不会出售。

No advertising or tracking / 无广告及追踪

Tongzhuo has no ads, no cross-app tracking and no third-party analytics SDK. The app does not request contacts or location access. Camera access is optional and used only when you photograph a receipt. The App Store version does not record audio.

同桌没有广告、没有跨应用追踪，也没有第三方分析 SDK。应用不请求通讯录或定位权限。相机权限是可选的，只在你拍摄收据时使用。App Store 版本不录音。

Children / 儿童

Tongzhuo is not directed to children, and online AI is not intended for anyone under the age at which they can consent to data processing where they live. A parent or guardian who believes a child has submitted personal information can contact us to have it removed.

同桌不面向儿童，线上 AI 也不面向未达到所在地区可自行同意数据处理年龄的用户。家长或监护人如认为儿童提交了个人信息，可联系我们删除。

Developer’s internal test builds / 开发者内部测试版

Internal TestFlight builds used only by the developer can also send voice recordings to OpenAI for transcription, under a separate permission. This feature is not part of the App Store version.

仅供开发者本人使用的内部 TestFlight 版本，还可以在另行同意后把录音发送给 OpenAI 转写。App Store 版本不包含这项功能。

你主动发送客服邮件时，我们会收到你的邮箱、正文和你选择的附件，仅用于回复和解决问题。客服使用 Gmail，邮件只在支持、跟进或法律义务所需的期间内保留。本网站托管在 GitHub Pages，未加入分析、Cookie 或追踪脚本；GitHub 可能出于安全目的记录 IP 地址。应用分发、系统诊断和内购由 Apple 按其政策和你的设置处理。
