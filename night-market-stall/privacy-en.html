MY LITTLE NIGHT STALL

Privacy Policy

Your game progress stays on your device.

English edition prepared: 2026-11-08 · Policy effective: 2026-11-07 · Little Everyday Apps

Who This Policy Covers

This policy applies to My Little Night Stall and its privacy and support website. The game is developed and operated by an independent developer using the publishing name Little Everyday Apps.

Offline Play, No Data Collection

The game requires no registration or sign-in. It does not send the developer personal information, device identifiers, gameplay records, or usage data. It contains no advertising, tracking, analytics, or third-party statistics SDKs, and does not request access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or location.

Stored Only on Your Device

Your coins, shift progress, neighbor stories, keepsakes, decorations, and sound settings are stored locally in the app. An unfinished shift is saved automatically and resumes in a paused state when you reopen the game. The game has no cloud sync. System backups may retain these records according to your device and Apple Account settings.

Saving, Deleting, and Recovery

Records remain until you delete the app's local data. If a save cannot be read, the game keeps a local copy of the original before starting a new save. This copy is not uploaded, and recovery is not guaranteed. To remove all local records, choose Delete App in system settings; Offload App keeps documents and data. Manage system backups in the relevant settings. The developer has no remote copy of your save.

When You Contact Support

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any files you choose to attach. We use them only to reply, investigate issues, or handle privacy requests, not for advertising or tracking. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary personal information. Support uses Gmail; Google processes messages under its own service terms and privacy policies. We keep emails for as long as needed to resolve your request, follow up, or meet applicable obligations, then delete them. You may request access, correction, or deletion from the email address you originally used to contact us. If retention is legally required, we will explain why. Service-provider backups follow the provider's own processes.

Website and System Services

The support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have added no ads, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, or cookies. GitHub may log information such as visitors' IP addresses for security; see its privacy statement. External websites have their own policies. Apple handles downloads, purchases (if applicable), system diagnostics, and device backups under its policies and your settings. These are not data collected by the game itself.

Players of Different Ages

The game does not actively collect personal data from players of any age. If a child provides personal information in a support email, a parent or guardian may contact the developer to request its deletion.

Policy Updates and Contact

Policy updates will show a date. If we add networking or data-processing features in the future, we will update this policy and the app's privacy disclosures. Publishing name: Little Everyday Apps. Privacy and support email: tripzy053@gmail.com.
