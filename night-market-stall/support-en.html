Support & Help

A little light, a warm skewer. Need a hand? Write to us.

English edition updated: 2026-11-09 · Policy effective: 2026-11-07 · Little Everyday Apps

Contact the Developer

Email tripzy053@gmail.com. Include your device model, operating system version, game version, and the steps leading up to the issue. Screenshots are optional. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary personal information. We reply by email.

Load, Flip, Serve

Drag a skewer from the prep tray to an empty grill spot, or tap the tray to load it automatically. When the grill button turns green, tap Flip. When it turns green again, tap Serve to hand the skewer to your guest. Your first order won't burn, and guests never rush you.

90 Seconds and Closing Up

Each shift has 90 seconds of active cooking time. Handoff animations and pauses don't use it up. Complete your goal to Close & Claim, or keep cooking. Missing a goal never costs you coins, and you keep the earnings you've made.

Unlocking Later Nights

Serve 3 guests, or cook for at least 60 seconds and serve at least one skewer, to unlock the next night. Start with one grill spot; a second opens on Night 3. From Night 4, you can choose a challenge to grill three perfect skewers in a row. Revisit unlocked nights in the Neighborhood Journal.

Neighbors and Keepsakes

A-He and A-Lan each have a three-part story. Serve a neighbor during a shift, then close up to record the next part. After the third part, you'll receive a Little House Lamp or a pot of Balcony Mint. Reread stories in the Neighborhood Journal and display or put away keepsakes in Decorate Your Stall.

Coins and Decorations

Coins are for in-game use only. There is no cash exchange, rewarded advertising, or in-app purchase. Buy decor and upgrades with your cooking earnings. Once owned, decorations can be displayed or put away anytime without paying again.

Music and Sound

The game respects Silent mode. Check your device's Silent mode, system volume, and sound controls in Settings on the home or pause screen. Music and effects have separate volume controls. Audio stops when the game goes into the background.

Changing the Language

Version 1.1 supports English and Simplified Chinese. Tap Language on the home screen, or open Settings from the home or pause screen. Choose 简体中文, English, or System. The language changes immediately without restarting, and your choice is remembered next time. Your coins, decorations, and story progress stay with your save.

Leaving and Returning

The game pauses and saves automatically in the background. Reopen it and choose Keep Cooking; the grill does not advance while you're away. Saves stay on this device, with no in-game cloud sync. Deleting the app removes local progress, so please don't delete it as your first troubleshooting step.

If a Save Cannot Be Read

The game keeps a local backup of the original save and starts a new record. Please contact support and describe what happened. The developer cannot access your save remotely, and recovery is not guaranteed.

Privacy and Deleting Records

No account or sign-in is required, and the app collects no personal data. Read the full Privacy Policy on this website or offline in the game's Privacy & Support screen.
