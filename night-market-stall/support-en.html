How to Cook

Look at the customer’s order card to see which skewer they want. Drag the matching skewer from its plate onto an empty grill spot, or tap the ingredient and then tap an empty spot. Tapping an ingredient on its own only selects it.

When the button turns green, tap Flip. When it turns green again, tap Serve. If you pick the wrong ingredient or miss the grill, the skewer goes back to its plate and you don’t lose any coins. When two customers are waiting, tap an order card to choose who to cook for.

Your first order of each shift can’t burn, and customers never walk away because they’ve waited too long.

If a Skewer Burns

Once a side is cooked, you have a few seconds before it burns. If it does, tap Clear Grill, then choose a fresh skewer for the order. The Steady-Heat Grill upgrade gives you a little more time before food burns.

Shifts and Closing Up

Each shift gives you 90 seconds of cooking time. Pausing and serving animations don’t count against it. Once you reach the night’s goal, tap Close & Claim to collect your reward, or keep cooking until time runs out. Missing a goal never costs you coins, and you keep everything you’ve earned.

Nine Nights

The game has nine nights. They’re chapters in the game, not real days, so you can play the next one right away. To unlock the next night, complete 3 orders, or stay open for 60 seconds and complete at least 1 order.

You start with one grill spot, and a second opens on Night 3. From Night 4, you can try an optional challenge: serve three perfectly grilled skewers in a row to earn 15 bonus coins. From Night 8, some customers order two skewers of the same kind. Cook them one at a time or use both grill spots; the order card shows how many you’ve served. From Night 9, customers can arrive in pairs, with a short break after each pair. You’ll never have to handle two double orders at once.

You can replay any unlocked night from the Neighborhood Journal. Each night’s goal reward can be claimed only once.

Neighbors and Keepsakes

A-He and A-Lan each have a three-part story. Complete their order during a shift, then close up to hear the next part. There’s no need to stop and tap through conversations. After the third part, you’ll receive a keepsake: a Little House Lamp or a pot of Balcony Mint.

Reread their stories in the Neighborhood Journal, or tap a keepsake on display. If you open a story during a shift, cooking pauses until you tap Keep Cooking. You can display or put away keepsakes in Decorate Your Stall.

Coins, Decorations, and Upgrades

Coins are earned only by playing and have no cash value. There are no ads and no in-app purchases. Spend coins on decorations and two upgrades: the Steady-Heat Grill gives you extra time before food burns, and the Glowing Sign earns you an extra coin per skewer. Decorations you own can be displayed or put away at any time for free.

No Sound?

The game follows your device’s Silent mode. Turn off Silent mode, turn up the volume, and check Music & Sound in Settings, which you can open from the home screen or the pause screen. Music and sound effects have separate volume controls. Sound stops when the game is in the background.

Changing the Language

The game is available in English and Simplified Chinese. Tap Language on the home screen, or open Settings from the home or pause screen, then choose English, 简体中文, or System. The change takes effect right away, with no restart needed, and the game remembers your choice. If your device uses a language the game doesn’t support, System shows English. Your coins, decorations, and story progress stay the same in either language.

Leaving and Coming Back

The game pauses and saves automatically when you leave. When you return, tap Keep Cooking. Nothing on the grill cooks while you’re away. Your progress is stored only on this device, and there is no cloud sync. Deleting the app deletes your progress, so please don’t delete and reinstall it as a first troubleshooting step.

If Your Save Can’t Be Loaded

The game keeps a copy of the original save on your device and starts a new one. Please email us and describe what happened. We can’t access saves remotely, so we can’t guarantee that progress can be recovered.

Privacy

No account or sign-in is needed, and the app doesn’t collect any personal data. Read the full Privacy Policy here, or offline in the game under Privacy & Support.
