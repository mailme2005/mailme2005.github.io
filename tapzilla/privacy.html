Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information the Tapzilla app, called "the App" here, uses, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. "We," "our" and "us" mean the developer of the App.

1. Summary

  • You don't need an account, name, email address or password to play Tapzilla.
  • Your progress, settings and saved performances stay on your device. We don't receive them.
  • The App doesn't use the microphone, camera, contacts or your precise location, and it never asks to track you across other apps and websites.
  • The App shows ads from Google, which are non-personalized. Buying Remove Ads turns ads off completely.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following on your device so the game works:

  • game progress, such as your player level, experience, scores, records, streaks and achievements;
  • your settings, such as instrument, color theme, sound and haptics;
  • performances you choose to save, which are the notes and timing of your taps;
  • whether you have bought Remove Ads.

This information isn't sent to us or synced between devices. It stays until you delete it in the App or delete the App. It may be included in your device backups, under Apple's terms.

3. Advertising

Unless you buy Remove Ads, the App shows ads using Google Mobile Ads, and asks for any privacy choices your region requires using Google's User Messaging Platform.

Every ad request the App makes:

  • asks for non-personalized ads, which are chosen from context, not from a profile of you;
  • asks Google to apply its child-directed treatment, which turns off personalized and interest-based advertising (from version 1.9);
  • limits ad content to Google's General Audiences rating.

The App never asks for permission to track you, so your device's advertising identifier isn't available to the App or to Google.

To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google and its partners may process limited information, such as your IP address, approximate location derived from it, device type and settings, ad interactions, and crash and performance data. Google handles this information under its own privacy policy.

4. Purchases

Apple processes the Remove Ads purchase under Apple's privacy policy. The App receives only confirmation of the purchase, so it can turn ads off. We never see your payment details.

5. Service Providers

  • Google (Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform): Google Privacy Policy, and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Apple (App Store and in-app purchases): Apple Privacy Policy.

We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with anyone else.

6. Your Choices and Rights

In the App, you can:

  • review or change your ad privacy choices in Settings > Ad Privacy Choices, where your region requires them;
  • buy Remove Ads to stop ads, and with them the information Google processes for ads;
  • delete saved performances, or delete the App to remove everything it stored on your device.

Your rights. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, to object to how it is used, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because we don't receive your gameplay information, most requests about ads are best made to Google. If you email us, we use your message only to reply, and you can ask us to delete it.

7. Children

Tapzilla is rated 4+ and can be enjoyed by players of all ages. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Because children may play, every ad request asks for child-directed treatment and general-audience content, as described above. If you are a parent or guardian with a question, please contact us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the App, our service providers or the law changes. We'll post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
