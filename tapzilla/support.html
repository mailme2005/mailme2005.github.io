Playing Tapzilla

How do I play?

Notes arrive on the beat. Tap each one as its gold ring closes, and it plays the next note of the song. Clean phrases bring in your band: bass, then pulse, then harmony. Chain 16 hits in a row to go On Fire, then keep going for Blazing and Legendary. Avoid the bombs.

What are the three modes?

Song Journey takes you through stages, each a song with its own rhythm. Miss a note and you lose a heart. Finish a phrase and you win one back.

Daily Jam is 60 seconds with a fresh goal every day. From version 1.10, every player gets the same jam, and a new one starts at midnight UTC. Complete the daily goal each day to build your streak.

Endless Jam speeds up as long as you keep up. The run ends when five notes in a row slip past.

What do "early" and "late" mean after a run?

They tell you whether most of your taps landed before or after the beat, so you know which way to adjust next time.

How do I hear my performance?

When you clear a Song Journey stage, Tapzilla plays your song back as a remix, at the pace you played it. Tap Save Performance to keep it, then open Saved on the Home screen to listen again. You can keep up to 50 performances.

How do I unlock more instruments?

Guitar, Piano and Marimba are ready from the start. Synth unlocks at Player Level 5, Bells at Level 10 and Bass at Level 15. Every run earns experience, and you can change instrument and color theme in Settings.

I can't hear any music.

Tapzilla follows your device's silent mode, so first check that the Ring/Silent switch or Action button isn't set to silent, and turn the volume up. Then open Settings in Tapzilla and make sure Music is on.

Do I need an internet connection or an account?

No. Tapzilla plays offline and doesn't need an account or sign-in. Ads need a connection to load. Game Center is optional. Tapzilla works on iPhone and iPad.

How do Game Center leaderboards and achievements work?

From version 1.10, sign in to Game Center in your device's Settings to join the Today's Jam, Endless · This Week and Endless · All-Time leaderboards and earn 15 achievements. Only Daily Jam and Endless Jam count. Song Journey and Flow Practice aren't part of Game Center.

Once you're signed in, tap Leaderboards on the Home screen to see your rank and your achievements. On iOS 26 or later, tap Challenge a Friend after a Daily Jam to dare a friend to beat your score.

My score isn't on the leaderboard.

Check that you're signed in to Game Center (Tapzilla's Settings shows it). If you played offline, your best score is sent the next time you open Tapzilla with a connection. A Daily Jam score counts only for the day it was played, and the Today's Jam board resets at midnight UTC.

Progress, purchases and ads

Where is my progress saved?

Your level, records, settings and saved performances are stored on your device. They aren't synced between devices, and deleting the app removes them. If you restore your iPhone or iPad from a backup, your progress comes back with it.

How do I remove ads?

Open Settings and tap Remove Ads. It's a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

I bought Remove Ads but I'm seeing ads on a new device.

Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you bought it with. Then open Settings in Tapzilla and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are made through Apple, so Apple handles refunds. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Where your region requires it, open Settings in Tapzilla and tap Ad Privacy Choices. Read our Privacy Policy for details of how ads work in Tapzilla.
