How do I scan a receipt?

  1. In Settings, turn on Claude receipt recognition and review the notice about uploading receipts.
  2. Take a photo or import one receipt. Keep it flat and well lit, with the merchant name and final total clearly visible.
  3. Check the merchant, date, currency, total, category and payment status, then confirm the expense.

Huayouju supports English and Chinese receipts. The default currency is SGD; you can change it for each entry. Recognition can make mistakes, so check the result against your receipt. Only confirmed, paid expenses count towards your totals, with each currency shown separately.

Cloud recognition needs an internet connection. With it turned off, you can still use on-device text recognition or enter expenses manually.

What do scan packs cost?

Start with 10 free AI scans per App Store account. If you need more, choose a one-time scan pack. There is no subscription or automatic renewal, and both free and purchased scans never expire.

When is a scan deducted?

One successfully processed receipt uses one scan, even if you decide not to use the result. If processing fails or no usable total is found, the scan is returned to your balance.

During processing, one scan may temporarily be unavailable. If the connection drops, allow time for the app to check the result and update your balance.

Viewing, editing, exporting or retrieving an existing result does not use another scan. Importing the same photo again may start a new scan, so check the original entry first.

What if my scan is interrupted?

  1. Keep the original entry and reconnect to the internet.
  2. Open the entry and tap Check original request. This checks for the existing result without starting or charging for a new scan.
  3. If processing is still underway, wait and check again later. Avoid importing the same photo again.

Unsaved cloud results are available to retrieve for up to 24 hours. Once saved on your phone, they are removed from our temporary cloud storage sooner. If a result is no longer available, you can complete the entry manually; the app will not automatically scan it again.

If the issue continues, contact us using the email below.

Purchases, new phones and refunds

Paid but your scans are missing? Use the same App Store account you used to buy the pack, then go to Settings → Scan balance and packs → Sync balance. You do not need to buy again after a connection problem. If Apple is still approving the purchase, sync again after it is approved.

Moving to a new phone? Your scan balance can be synced with the same App Store account. Reinstalling the app does not restart the free trial. Expense records and receipt photos are stored separately on your phone, so export a full backup before switching devices.

Need a refund? Request one through Apple’s Report a Problem website. Apple reviews refund requests. Scans from a refunded pack are removed from your balance. If you have already used some of them, a future purchase may first cover those used scans.

How do I back up my expenses?

In Settings, open the backup and export section and choose Save full backup. Save the file somewhere you can access if you lose or replace your phone. It includes your expenses, receipt photos and recognition notes.

Choose Restore from backup to preview and add records from a backup. Entries already present are kept, and restored receipts are not automatically sent for AI recognition. Use CSV export if you want to work with your expenses in a spreadsheet; CSV files do not include photos or restore a full backup.

The app keeps recent recovery copies on your phone to help recover damaged records. These do not protect against a lost phone or deleting the app. Save a full backup before reinstalling or troubleshooting.

Languages and supported receipts

Huayouju follows your phone’s language by default. In Settings, you can choose 中文 or English, or select System to follow your phone again. Existing AI notes stay in the language in which they were created.

Scan one purchase at a time. Bank statements, credit-card statements and refunds are not automatically split into separate expenses. Huayouju does not connect to your bank, transfer money or pay bills.
