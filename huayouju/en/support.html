From photo to expense

Photograph or import one complete, legible receipt. In Settings, enable Claude receipt recognition and accept the upload notice to have new receipts processed with cloud AI. Check the merchant, date, currency, total, category and payment status before confirming an entry. On-device recognition and manual entry remain available with Claude disabled.

Keep the receipt flat and evenly lit, with the merchant and final total in frame. English and Chinese receipts are supported. The default currency is SGD and can be changed per entry. Recognition can make mistakes; check the original receipt. Totals include only confirmed, paid expenses and are grouped by currency.

Free trial and scan packs

Each App Store account receives 10 free scans. Optional packs are one-time purchases with no subscription, automatic renewal or expiry. Neither free nor purchased scans have an expiry date.

When is a scan used?

One successfully processed receipt uses one scan. Technical failures or results without a usable total return the reserved scan. An interrupted connection may leave a scan marked as processing while the outcome is checked; a hold is released if processing cannot complete.

Viewing, editing, exporting and retrieving an existing result use no additional scans. A successfully generated result uses a scan even if you choose not to apply it. Importing the same photo again may start a new scan, so check the original entry first.

Interrupted or missing results

Keep the original entry. Once connected, open it and choose Check original request. This retrieves the existing request rather than making another Claude call. Wait if asked to try later, and avoid repeatedly importing the photo. Review a retrieved result before saving.

Cloud results can be retrieved for up to 24 hours and are cleared earlier after the phone acknowledges saving them. Expiry does not trigger another scan. You can continue editing the local entry by hand. If the service is unavailable, try later or contact support.

Missing purchases, new devices and refunds

Use the same App Store account that made the purchase, then open Settings → Scan balance and packs → Sync balance. Do not buy again if the connection failed after payment. Purchases awaiting Apple approval are credited after approval and synchronization.

Your scan balance follows your App Store account; reinstalling does not grant another trial. Expense records and receipt photos do not automatically sync with the balance. Export a full backup before moving devices.

Apple handles purchase refunds through Report a Problem. Refunded packs are deducted from your balance. If some refunded scans were already used, later top-ups may first cover that shortfall.

Backups, exports and recovery

Choose Settings → Backup & export → Save full backup to save entries, original photos and recognition notes somewhere you trust. Restore from backup previews and merges records, preserves existing entries with the same identifier, and does not upload old receipts automatically. CSV export is suitable for spreadsheets but contains no photos and cannot restore the full ledger.

The app keeps up to five recent local snapshots for ledger recovery. These share the same phone and original images, so they do not protect against losing the phone or deleting the app. Back up before troubleshooting; do not start by deleting the app.

Language and current limits

The interface follows your device language by default. In Settings, select 中文, English or System. AI notes in older entries remain in the language in which they were generated.

Record individual purchases. Bank statements, credit-card statements and refunds cannot currently be split into multiple entries automatically. Huayouju does not connect to your bank, transfer money or pay bills.
