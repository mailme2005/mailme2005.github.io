Scope

This policy covers the Huayouju (花有据) iPhone app and this support website. The developer and operator is Guan Jie Tan, publishing as Little Everyday Apps. Privacy contact: tripzy053@gmail.com.

Version 0.7.0 is currently in private TestFlight testing with cloud recognition and sandbox scan-pack purchases. Live App Store paid services are not yet open, and test balances are separate from live balances. This policy describes the current implementation.

Records and photos on your device

Receipt photos, recognized text, merchant, date, amount, currency, category, payment and review status, recognition notes, and language and usage preferences are stored locally. On-device recognition uses your device’s text recognition. With Claude disabled, receipts are not uploaded to our cloud for recognition.

The app also keeps unfinished processing state and up to five recent ledger snapshots to prevent repeat processing and recover a damaged ledger. Ledger files use iOS file protection. System backups may contain local data, depending on your Apple settings. We do not provide cloud synchronization or remote recovery of the full ledger or original photos.

Optional Claude cloud recognition

The app asks for consent before enabling Claude. Once enabled, newly captured or imported receipts send a compressed image and locally recognized text through Huayouju’s Google Cloud service to Anthropic’s Claude API to organize expense information. Images or text may contain names, addresses, merchants, purchased items, amounts, membership numbers or payment information. Re-encoding does not copy the source photo’s metadata, but information visible in the image or recognized text is still uploaded.

Turn off Claude receipt recognition in Settings to stop future cloud submissions. Requests already sent cannot be recalled. On-device recognition and manual entry remain available. We do not connect to your bank or ask for card numbers, but receipts you choose to upload may contain that information.

Cloud processing and retention

Huayouju processes uploaded images and text without persistently saving the source image or raw OCR text in its request ledger. Extracted receipt results are temporarily stored for recovery: they are retrievable for up to 24 hours and deleted earlier after the phone acknowledges saving them. Expired results are no longer served; background storage cleanup follows. This is not a permanent cloud backup.

Request identifiers, content hashes, status, timestamps, model and cost/usage records remain to prevent repeated calls. The scan-pack version also associates requests with a pseudonymous account and records credit reservations and settlement for balances and disputes. These records do not have the result cache’s 24-hour expiry.

Anthropic’s standard API policy generally deletes inputs and outputs within 30 days, with exceptions including safety enforcement, legal obligations, covered-model requirements or separate agreements. We do not claim a zero-retention agreement. Anthropic does not train on commercial API inputs or outputs by default; explicit feedback or opt-in sharing may be exceptions. See its API retention policy and training policy.

App Store identity, balances and purchases

When checking balances or processing purchases, the app sends Apple-signed app and purchase credentials to Huayouju for verification. You do not create a separate Huayouju account or give us your Apple password. We do not request your name or email for this process. Credentials contain app, account-association, environment and transaction information.

The server derives a pseudonymous account reference from the app transaction identifier and stores free and purchased balances, reserved scans, an app account token, product and transaction identifiers, verification timestamps and refund state. These deliver the trial once, fulfill purchases, prevent duplicate credit, synchronize balances and handle refunds. Apple processes payments; Apple does not send us your payment-card number.

Free and purchased scans do not expire. Balance, trial-claim, transaction and replay-prevention records remain as needed to provide scans, handle refunds and disputes, prevent abuse or meet applicable obligations. Deleting the app does not erase these server records or grant another trial.

Service providers and processing locations

Google Cloud hosts Huayouju’s service and credit ledger, with the current service and database located in Singapore. Anthropic processes AI requests you choose to submit, and Apple provides app distribution and payments. Providers may also process IP addresses, app or device information, security events and operational logs under their policies. Processing may occur outside your country or region.

This information supports functionality, security and troubleshooting. Huayouju has no advertising, cross-app tracking or third-party analytics SDK, and does not sell personal information. The app does not request contacts, location or microphone access.

Permissions, exports and deletion

The camera is used when you choose to capture a receipt; manage permission in iOS Settings. Image import uses the system picker for photos you select. When exporting a full backup, CSV or shared file, you choose its destination or recipient. Full backups contain original receipt photos and expense records, so store them securely.

You can delete entries in the app. Deleted entries and associated photos may remain in recent local recovery snapshots until those snapshots rotate out. Deleting the app and its data removes local app files; iOS Offload App retains data. Exported files, system photos, shared content and device backups must be managed separately.

For access, correction or deletion of cloud-service, purchase or support data, contact our privacy email. The current version has no in-app cloud-account deletion button. We may verify the relevant account or transaction and explain what can be deleted and the minimum records needed to preserve purchased scans, resolve disputes, prevent repeat trials or meet legal obligations. Do not send Apple passwords or complete signed credentials. Deleting local entries does not request an Apple refund. Provider logs and backups follow their own retention and cleanup processes.

Support email and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and chosen attachments to respond, troubleshoot and handle your request. Support uses Gmail. Messages and attachments are retained as needed for support, follow-up or applicable obligations and deleted when no longer needed. Redact unrelated personal information from receipts first.

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages without added analytics scripts, tracking pixels, ads, cookies or external fonts. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security. External links are subject to the destination provider’s policies.

Contact, children’s information and updates

A parent or guardian may contact tripzy053@gmail.com for help or deletion if a child submits personal information. Use the same address for privacy access, correction, deletion and other questions.

We will update the date when this policy changes. Material changes to data handling will be reflected here and in relevant in-app notices, with renewed consent where needed.
