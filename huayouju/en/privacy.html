About this policy

This policy explains how the Huayouju (花有据) iPhone app and this support website handle your information. Huayouju is developed and operated by Guan Jie Tan under Little Everyday Apps.

Your expense records and original receipt photos are stored on your phone. If you enable AI recognition, receipt images and text are sent for cloud processing. We also keep the records needed to provide your free scans, purchases and scan balance.

For privacy questions or requests, email tripzy053@gmail.com.

Information stored on your phone

The app stores your receipt photos, recognized text, expense details, review and payment status, AI notes, and preferences on your device. Expense details include merchants, dates, amounts, currencies and categories.

On-device text recognition runs on your phone. When Claude receipt recognition is off, receipts are not uploaded to our service for recognition.

The app keeps the progress of unfinished scans and up to five recent recovery copies of your records. Local files use iOS file protection and may be included in device backups, depending on your Apple settings. Your full expense history and original photos are not automatically synced to our cloud or available for us to restore remotely.

When you use AI recognition

Before you enable Claude receipt recognition, the app asks for your consent to upload receipts. Once enabled, new receipts you photograph or import are sent as compressed images, together with text recognized on your phone, through our Google Cloud service to Anthropic, the provider of Claude. This information is used to extract and organize the expense details.

Receipts may contain names, addresses, purchased items, membership numbers and payment information. The uploaded image does not include the original photo’s metadata, but information visible in the receipt is still sent.

You can turn off Claude receipt recognition in Settings at any time to stop future uploads. This cannot recall information already sent. You can continue using on-device recognition and manual entry. Huayouju does not connect to your bank.

How long cloud information is kept

Receipt images and text: our service processes them without saving the uploaded image or original recognized text as part of its lasting scan records. Anthropic’s separate retention rules are explained below.

Recognition results: we temporarily store the extracted receipt details so you can retrieve a result after a connection problem. Results are available for up to 24 hours and are deleted sooner when your phone confirms they have been saved. After expiry, results are no longer accessible and are removed during storage cleanup. This temporary storage is not a backup of your expenses.

Usage records: we keep a scan reference, a digital fingerprint of the submitted content, processing status, times, AI model, usage and cost information, and the associated scan-balance record. These help prevent duplicate processing and charges, resolve problems and manage your balance. They are retained beyond the 24-hour result window for these purposes.

Anthropic: under its standard API policy, inputs and outputs are generally deleted within 30 days. Some information may be kept longer for safety, legal reasons, model-specific requirements or separate agreements. Commercial API information is not used to train its models by default; explicit feedback or opt-in sharing may be exceptions. See Anthropic’s retention policy and model training policy.

Free scans, purchases and your balance

You do not need to create a separate Huayouju account. To check your balance and verify purchases, the app sends Apple-issued app and purchase records to our service. We use an account reference linked to your App Store use, rather than requesting your name, email address or Apple password.

We store your free and purchased scan balances, scans being processed, product and transaction references, verification times and refund status. This lets us provide the free trial once, deliver purchased scans, sync your balance and handle refunds without crediting the same purchase twice. Apple handles payment and does not provide us with your payment-card number.

Free and purchased scans do not expire. We retain the account, trial, purchase and usage records needed to maintain your balance, handle refunds or disputes, prevent misuse and meet applicable obligations. Deleting the app does not delete these records or restart your trial. You can contact us about deleting cloud data as described below.

Who processes your information

We use the following providers to deliver the app and support:

  • Google Cloud hosts our service and scan-balance records in Singapore.
  • Anthropic processes the receipts you submit for Claude recognition.
  • Apple distributes the app and handles purchases.
  • Google / Gmail handles support email, and GitHub Pages hosts this website.

These providers may process IP addresses, app or device information, security events and service logs under their own policies. Processing may take place outside your country or region, including when our service is hosted in Singapore.

Huayouju does not sell personal information or use advertising, cross-app tracking or third-party analytics tools. It does not request access to your contacts, location or microphone.

Your choices and deletion requests

Camera and photos: the app uses the camera when you choose to photograph a receipt. You can manage camera permission in iOS Settings. Importing photos uses Apple’s photo picker for images you select.

Exports and backups: you choose where to save or share exported files. Full backups contain expense records and original receipt photos; keep them somewhere secure.

Deleting local records: you can delete expenses in the app. Deleted records and related photos may remain in recent recovery copies until these are replaced. Deleting the app and its data removes local app files; iOS Offload App keeps data. Photos in your photo library, exported files, shared copies and device backups must be managed separately.

Cloud and support data: email tripzy053@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion. We may ask for information to verify the relevant account or purchase. We will explain any effect on your remaining scans and any records we need to retain for purchases, disputes, misuse prevention or legal obligations. Never send your Apple password or verification codes.

Deleting an expense does not request a purchase refund. Service-provider logs and backups follow their own retention and deletion processes.

Support email and this website

When you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to include. We use them to respond, investigate issues and handle your request. We keep this information for as long as needed for support, follow-up or applicable obligations, then delete it. Please hide unrelated personal information before sending receipts or screenshots.

This website does not add cookies, advertising or visitor analytics. GitHub Pages records visitor IP addresses for security. If you follow an external link, the destination website’s privacy policy applies.

Children’s information and policy updates

If a child has shared personal information with us, a parent or guardian can email tripzy053@gmail.com for help or to request deletion.

We will update the date on this page when this policy changes. Important changes to how we handle your information will also be explained in the app where relevant, and we will ask for renewed consent when needed.
