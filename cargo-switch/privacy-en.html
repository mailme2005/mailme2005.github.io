Version coverage

This policy covers Cargo Switch 1.2. Earlier versions do not offer Apple In-App Purchases.

Game data stays on your device

No game account is required. Gameplay works offline. The app contains no ads, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs. Progress, board positions, tutorial progress, coins, tokens and recent activity stay on your device and are not uploaded to the developer.

Optional Apple purchases

Version 1.2 offers optional consumable coin purchases through Apple. Apple processes payment under its own terms and privacy policy. The app receives transaction details from StoreKit, verifies them and stores transaction identifiers, product identifiers, credited amounts and refund status locally to prevent duplicate delivery and handle refunds. The developer does not receive your payment card details or upload this local transaction ledger to a server.

Keeping and deleting your save

Coins do not expire. Your wallet and progress are local; the developer has no server copy or cloud-sync service. Deleting the app may delete them. Completed consumable purchases cannot be restored after deletion or on another device. Device backup behavior depends on your settings.

Support emails

If you contact tripzy053@gmail.com, your email provider and the developer receive the address, message and attachments you send. These are used to respond to your request. Do not send passwords, card details or unrelated personal information. Contact the same address to request deletion of support emails.

This website

This site uses GitHub Pages with no analytics scripts, ads or forms. The hosting provider may process IP addresses, browser information and access logs to operate the site. This is separate from data handling in the game.
