Version coverage

This policy covers Cargo Switch 1.2 and later. Earlier versions do not offer Apple In-App Purchases.

Where game data is kept

No game account is required. Gameplay works offline. The app contains no ads, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs. Progress, board positions, tutorial progress, coins, tokens and recent activity are saved on your device and are not uploaded to the developer. In versions 1.4 and later, when the device is signed in to iCloud, your unlocked and completed levels, best move counts, coins, token counts and purchase records also sync to your own iCloud account so they can be restored after you reinstall the game or move to another device. Apple stores that data under your iCloud settings, and the developer cannot read it. Board positions, undo history and recent activity do not sync.

Optional Apple purchases

Versions 1.2 and later offer optional consumable coin purchases through Apple. Apple processes payment under its own terms and privacy policy. The app receives transaction details from StoreKit, verifies them and stores transaction identifiers, product identifiers, credited amounts and refund status locally to prevent duplicate delivery and handle refunds. The developer does not receive your payment card details or upload this transaction ledger to a server of the developer. Versions 1.4 and later sync the ledger to your own iCloud together with the wallet.

Keeping and deleting your save

Coins do not expire. The developer runs no server and has no copy of your save. Versions 1.4 and later: when the device is signed in to iCloud, your wallet and progress sync to your iCloud and are restored automatically after you reinstall the game or open it on another device signed in to the same Apple Account. Without iCloud they are saved only on the device, and deleting the app deletes them. Versions 1.3 and earlier save only on the device; deleting the app may delete the save, and completed consumable purchases cannot be restored. Device backup behavior depends on your settings.

Support emails

If you contact tripzy053@gmail.com, your email provider and the developer receive the address, message and attachments you send. These are used to respond to your request. Do not send passwords, card details or unrelated personal information. Contact the same address to request deletion of support emails.

This website

This site uses GitHub Pages with no analytics scripts, ads or forms. The hosting provider may process IP addresses, browser information and access logs to operate the site. This is separate from data handling in the game.
