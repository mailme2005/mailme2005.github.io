Offline play and local data

Cargo Switch requires no account, sign-in, or internet connection. It has no ads, in-app purchases, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. Gameplay actions, coin activity, and playtest feedback are not uploaded automatically.

What is saved on your device

Level progress, best move counts, current crate and gate positions, undo history, tutorial progress, coins, tokens, and recent transactions are saved on your device to restore your game. Game coins have no cash value and cannot be bought with real money, cashed out, or transferred.

Keeping and deleting data

The developer has no copy of your save data and provides no online account. Deleting the app deletes its local save data. Whether backups and restores include this data depends on your device settings. Offloading the app while keeping its documents and data may preserve your save.

Support emails

If you choose to email support, your email provider and the developer receive the email address, message, and attachments you provide. This information is used only to handle your request. Please do not send passwords or unrelated sensitive information. You can ask for related support emails to be deleted by contacting the support address.

This support website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no analytics scripts, ads, or user forms. The hosting provider may process IP addresses, browser information, and access logs to provide the website. Its handling of this information is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. This is separate from data handling inside the offline game.
