Privacy policy

Updated November 19, 2026

In one sentence

Cargo Switch works offline, needs no account, has no ads and does not track you. Your progress and coins stay on your device and in your own iCloud, where we cannot see them.

What we receive

The game itself sends us nothing about you. We only receive your email address and your message if you choose to write to us.

When you play

Your level progress, best move counts, coins and tokens are saved on your device. The game has no ads, does not track you and gathers no usage statistics.

When you buy coins

Coins are optional. Apple handles the payment, and we never see your card or Apple Account details. The game only notes which coins have been delivered and which were refunded, so nothing is delivered twice.

When you contact us

We receive your email address, your message and any files you attach, and use them to reply, solve the problem and handle your request. Please don’t send passwords or card details.

Who helps handle it

  • Apple handles payments and refunds, and keeps your game save when iCloud is on.
  • Email (Gmail) is used only when you write to us.
  • This website has no ads and no analytics scripts. Its host, GitHub, may keep basic visit records such as IP addresses.

Their own policies: Apple privacy policy · Google privacy policy · GitHub privacy statement

Where it is kept and how to delete it

Where it is kept

Your save is on your device. If the device is signed in to iCloud, your progress, best move counts, coins, tokens and coin delivery records are also saved to your own iCloud, so you can get them back after reinstalling or changing devices. Apple keeps that copy under your iCloud settings, and we cannot read it.

We run no server and hold no copy of your save, so we cannot view or restore it for you.

How to delete it

  • Deleting the game deletes the save on your device. If iCloud is on, the copy in your own iCloud stays, and your progress and coins come back when you reinstall with the same Apple Account.
  • Only the game can read the iCloud copy. We cannot see it or delete it for you. The game does not yet have an option to clear it; if you want it removed, please write to us.
  • To have your emails with us deleted, just write and ask.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from players of any age. If a child has sent us personal information by email, a parent or guardian can write to us to have it deleted. Coin purchases go through the Apple Account, and parents can manage them with Ask to Buy or Screen Time.

For any privacy question, email tripzy053@gmail.com.
