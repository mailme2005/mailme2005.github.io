How to play

How do I move the crates?

Horizontal crates slide left and right; vertical crates slide up and down. Slide each colored crate along a clear row and out through its matching exit on the right. Gray crates only need to move out of the way; they stay on the board.

Each drag counts as one move, however far you slide. You can also select a crate and use the arrow buttons or Deliver.

How does the shifting gate work?

The dark bar with yellow stripes is where the gate is now. The dashed outline is where it will go next. Shifting costs one move. If that space is occupied, the gate cannot shift and no move is used. Levels 6 and 7 have step-by-step tutorials that you can skip or replay.

What if I get stuck?

Undo and Restart are free, and you can replay any unlocked level. If you run out of moves, switch to Practice for unlimited moves; hints there cost the same as in normal play. There is no timer.

How do levels unlock?

There are 60 levels in 7 chapters. Clear a level to unlock the next one; clearing it in Practice counts too.

How is my best move count set?

Only a normal clear without hints or extra moves updates your best move count. Practice does not count.

Coins and purchases

Where do coins come from, and what are they for?

  • You start with 300 coins and earn 30 more the first time you clear each level normally. Practice and replays give none.
  • A hint costs 60 coins. Three extra moves cost 120 coins, up to three times per attempt.
  • You can also exchange coins for a Hint Token (60 coins) or an Extra Moves Token (120 coins) to use later.
  • Every level can be unlocked for free by playing. Coins never expire and have no cash value.

Can I buy coins?

Yes, and it is entirely optional. Where available there are packs of 600, 1000 and 2400 coins. You see the local price before you confirm, and Apple handles the payment.

When are coins used for hints and extra moves?

A hint uses coins or a Hint Token only after it has found your next move. If you cancel, the position has no solution, or no answer is found, it costs nothing. You can view the same hint again for free during that attempt.

Extra moves apply only to the current attempt. Restarting or changing levels clears them, along with hints unlocked in that attempt; unused tokens are kept. Hints are free in tutorials. In Practice, hints cost the same as in normal play, and extra moves are not offered.

I paid but the coins are missing

  • Please don’t buy the pack again or delete the game.
  • Connect to the internet, reopen the game, tap your coin balance and choose Check for Missing Coins.
  • A purchase that is waiting for approval is delivered only after Apple approves it.

If the coins are still missing, email us your game version, roughly when you bought, and the name of the pack. Include your Apple order number if you can, and cover anything unrelated.

I canceled, or my purchase is still pending

Canceling a purchase adds no coins. A pending or interrupted payment adds coins only once it succeeds; reopen the game afterward to receive them.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. Once a refund goes through, the game takes back the matching coins, once, and your balance never goes below zero.

Progress, new devices and reinstalling

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. If the device is signed in to iCloud, your progress, coins and tokens also sync to your own iCloud. Tap your coin balance; the note under Buy Coins says whether syncing is on.

Will my progress survive a new phone or a reinstall?

With iCloud syncing on, open the game on a device signed in to the same Apple Account and your progress and coins come back automatically. It can take a moment and needs an internet connection. A level you are in the middle of, and its undo history, do not sync.

With iCloud syncing on, deleting and reinstalling the game brings your progress back too. To start over, tap Delete Save from iCloud in Settings, then delete and reinstall the game. Purchased coins are cleared too and cannot be restored.

What if I don’t use iCloud?

Your progress and coins stay on this device only. Deleting the game deletes them, and purchased coins cannot be restored.

Before you delete the game or change devices, update it to the latest version and open it once while online, then wait a moment.

Can Check for Missing Coins bring back my save?

No. It only delivers purchases that have not arrived yet; it is not a cloud backup.

Language and system requirements

How do I change the language?

Tap 中文 / English on the main screen and choose a language in Settings.

Why doesn’t my game have these features?

The game needs iOS 18 or iPadOS 18 or later. Devices on older systems keep an earlier version with fewer levels and features, and it does not sync to iCloud.
