How do I play?

Horizontal crates slide left and right; vertical crates slide up and down. Each drag counts as one move, no matter the distance. You can also select a crate and use the arrow buttons to move it. Slide each colored crate along a clear row and out through its matching exit on the right. Gray crates only need to move out of the way; they stay on the board.

How does the shifting gate work?

The solid yellow-striped barrier marks the gate's current position. The dashed outline shows where it will move next. Shifting costs one move. If that space is occupied, the gate cannot shift and no move is used. Levels 6 and 7 include optional tutorials that you can replay.

What if I get stuck?

Undo, retry, or choose another level for free. If you run out of moves, enter Practice for unlimited moves and free hints. There is no timer.

Do coins or tokens cost real money?

No. You start with 300 game coins. Your first clear of each level outside Practice earns 30 coins. A hint costs 60 coins or one Hint Token; 3 extra moves cost 120 coins or one Move Token. You can add moves up to three times per attempt. Spend game coins in the shop to get tokens; no real payment or ads are involved.

When are coins or tokens used?

A hint uses coins or a token only after the next move is successfully found. Cancelled searches, unsolvable positions, and searches that reach the search limit cost nothing. The same unlocked hint is free to view again during that attempt. Extra moves apply only to the current attempt. Restarting or changing levels clears extra moves and unlocked hints for that attempt. Unused tokens stay in your inventory.

Progress and best move counts

All levels are unlocked. Progress is saved on your device. Practice and assisted attempts do not update your best unassisted move count. Replaying a cleared level gives no additional first-clear reward. The developer has no remote copy of your save and cannot restore deleted local data.

Report an issue

Email tripzy053@gmail.com with your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, game version, level number, and the actions that led to the issue. You may include a screenshot. Do not send passwords or sensitive information.

Choose a language

Tap “中文 / English” on the main screen to open Settings, then choose English or 简体中文 (Simplified Chinese). Changes apply immediately and are saved for next time. Switching languages does not reset your progress, coins, or tokens.
