Play and unlock

Version 1.2 has 50 levels. Clear each level to unlock the next; free Practice clears count too. Earlier versions may have fewer levels or a different level-selection system. Version 1.2 requires iOS 18 or iPadOS 18 or later; devices on iOS 17 keep version 1.1. Undo, retry and Practice are free. Use Settings to switch between English and Simplified Chinese.

How do I play?

Horizontal crates slide left and right; vertical crates slide up and down. Each drag counts as one move, no matter the distance. You can also select a crate and use the arrow buttons or Deliver to move it. Slide each colored crate along a clear row and out through its matching exit on the right. Gray crates only need to move out of the way; they stay on the board.

How does the shifting gate work?

The solid yellow-striped barrier marks the gate's current position. The dashed outline shows where it will move next. Shifting costs one move. If that space is occupied, the gate cannot shift and no move is used. Levels 6 and 7 include optional tutorials that you can replay.

What if I get stuck?

Undo and retry are free, and you can replay any unlocked level. If you run out of moves, switch to Practice for unlimited moves and free hints; clearing a level in Practice also unlocks the next one. There is no timer.

Coins and tools

In version 1.2, 600 Coins is an optional Apple In-App Purchase where available. Apple shows the local price before you confirm. A hint costs 60 coins; three extra moves cost 120 coins, at most three times per attempt. All levels remain free to unlock through play. Coins never expire and have no cash value.

When are coins or tokens used?

A hint uses coins or a Hint Token only after the next move is successfully found. Canceled searches, unsolvable positions, and searches that reach the search limit cost nothing. The same unlocked hint is free to view again during that attempt. Extra moves apply only to the current attempt. Restarting or changing levels clears extra moves and unlocked hints for that attempt; unused tokens stay in your inventory. Hints are free in tutorials and Practice, where extra moves are not offered.

Paid but coins are missing

Do not buy the pack again or delete the game. Connect to the internet, reopen the app, tap your coin balance and choose Check for Missing Coins. Pending purchases are credited only after Apple approves them. If coins are still missing, email support with the app version, approximate purchase time, product name and Apple order reference if available. Redact unrelated details. Never send passwords or payment card information.

Canceled or pending purchases

Canceling the Apple purchase sheet does not add coins. A pending approval or interrupted payment does not add coins until the purchase succeeds. Reopen the app after approval or resolution to receive the coins.

Refunds

Apple handles refund requests. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request help with an Apple purchase. When Apple reports a refunded transaction, the game adjusts the available coin balance once; it does not make the balance negative.

Progress and best move counts

You start with 300 coins and earn 30 coins for your first clear of each level outside Practice; replaying a cleared level gives no additional reward. Practice and assisted attempts do not update your best unassisted move count.

Local saves

Progress, coins and tokens stay on this device. There is no cloud-sync service. Deleting the app may lose them, and completed consumable purchases cannot be restored on another device or after deletion. Check for Missing Coins only checks unfinished purchases; it is not a cloud backup.
