Frequently asked questions Current app

Is this the same app as Tripzy 3?

Yes. Tripzy 3: Beyond Boundaries is now called Tripzy: AI Trip Planner. It's the same app: your saved trips and credits stay as they are, and there's nothing to reinstall.

How do credits work?

Tripzy uses credits instead of a subscription. A trip of up to 5 days uses 1 credit, 6 to 10 days uses 2 credits, and 11 to 21 days uses 3 credits. New users receive free credits to get started, once per Apple Account. You can buy packs of 3, 10 or 20 credits in the app at any time.

What happens if my trip can't be created?

Credits are used only for a plan you receive. If something goes wrong while your trip is being planned, the credits set aside for it are returned to your balance automatically. If they don't come back, email us and we'll help.

I have a new iPhone or iPad. Will my credits move?

Yes. Install Tripzy on the new device while signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account, then open it. Your credits are reconnected automatically. Trips are saved on each device, so trips from your old device don't move across.

How do I get a refund for a purchase?

Purchases are made through Apple, so Apple handles refunds. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How long can a trip be, and how long does planning take?

Trips can be up to 21 days. Planning usually takes a few minutes. For the best result, keep Tripzy open until your plan is ready.

Can I make the plan more personal?

Yes. When you plan a trip, open Must-sees, hotel and food to add places you must see, a hotel you've already booked, a food preference (vegetarian, vegan or halal) and, on a family trip, the age of your youngest child. These are all optional.

Can Tripzy choose the cities for me?

Yes. Enter a country as your destination, and Tripzy asks how you'd like to explore it: A few cities, One base, with day trips, or Suggest for me. Tripzy then picks the stops and splits your days between them.

Can I share or print my plan?

Yes. Open a trip and use Export PDF to create a travel guide you can print or share. To share from your trip list, press and hold a trip and choose Share.

How do I delete a trip?

On your trip list, press and hold the trip and choose Delete. Trips are stored on your device, so deleting a trip removes it from that device.

Can I use Tripzy on my iPad?

Yes. Tripzy works on both iPhone and iPad.

What information does Tripzy use?

You don't need an account, name or email address. Your trip details are used only to plan your trip. Read our Privacy Policy for the full details.

Tripzy! (Original) Retired

The original subscription-based travel planner has been retired. It's no longer available on the App Store, and its Pro and Pro+ subscriptions can no longer be bought or renewed.

I used the original Tripzy! app. What should I use now?

Please use Tripzy: AI Trip Planner for new trips. It's a separate app, built from the ground up, with no subscription: you buy credits only when you need them, and new users receive free credits to get started.

What happens to the trips I saved in the original app?

If the original app is still on your device, you can keep opening it to view the trips saved there. Trips can't be moved between the two apps.

Still need help?

Email us and we'll get back to you as soon as we can. To help us help you faster, include your app version and device model. You'll find both in the app under Send Feedback.
