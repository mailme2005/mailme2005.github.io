Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information the Tripzy: AI Trip Planner app (formerly "Tripzy 3: Beyond Boundaries"), called "the App" here, collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. "Tripzy," "we," "our" and "us" mean the developer of the App.

1. Summary

  • You don't need an account, name, email address or password to use Tripzy.
  • We don't sell your information, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.
  • To create your trip plan, your trip details are sent to the service providers listed below, including an AI service. Our servers keep them for no more than about two days.
  • Trips you save stay on your device.
  • You can contact us at any time to ask about, correct or delete your information.

2. Information We Collect

Information you give us

  • Trip details: where you're traveling from and to, your dates, travel style, interests, timing choices, and any notes you add.
  • Optional personal details: places you must see, a hotel you've booked, a food preference (vegetarian, vegan or halal), and the age range of the youngest child traveling with you. You can leave all of these blank.
  • Feedback and messages: anything you choose to include in feedback, which also carries the App version, iOS version and device model, and your email address and message if you write to us.

Information collected automatically

  • An anonymous account: the App creates a random account identifier the first time you open it. It isn't linked to your name or email address.
  • Security checks: information that confirms requests come from a genuine copy of the App on a genuine Apple device.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, a token that lets us send them to your device.
  • Service records: technical details such as request identifiers, times, error types and usage limits, which we need to run the service and prevent misuse.
  • Anonymous usage totals: daily totals of how often features are used, for example how many trips were opened, shared or rated "Helpful" or "Not for me". The totals contain no trip details, places or identifiers. You can turn them off in the App under Send Feedback → Privacy → Share anonymous usage counts.

Purchases and credits

  • Apple handles all payments. We never receive your card or bank details.
  • We receive Apple's confirmation of your purchases and keep your credit balance and its history.
  • To give free credits only once per Apple Account, and to restore your credits on a new device, we keep a coded identifier made from your App Store purchase information. It can't be turned back into your Apple Account details.

What we don't collect: your name, precise location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifier. The App contains no advertising or third-party analytics.

3. How We Use Information

  • To plan your trips, including places, routes and travel information. This is the service you ask us for.
  • To run credits and purchases: checking purchases, keeping your balance, restoring credits and preventing double grants. This is also needed to provide the service, and for our accounting and legal obligations.
  • To keep the service secure and reliable: preventing fraud and misuse, enforcing usage limits, and fixing problems. We rely on our legitimate interest in running a safe service.
  • To improve the App, using anonymous usage totals and the feedback you send. We rely on our legitimate interest in improving the App, and you can turn usage totals off.
  • To send notifications you've allowed, and to reply to your messages.

Sensitive information. A halal food preference may reveal religious beliefs. We use it only to plan meals for that trip, and only if you choose to add it. By adding it, you agree to that use. You can plan without it at any time.

We don't make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects. The AI service suggests a trip plan, which you are free to use or ignore.

4. Service Providers and Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run the App, and only what they need to do that work. They may use it only to provide their services to us, and they must protect it at least as well as this policy describes.

  • Google Cloud (Firebase): hosts our servers and stores and processes the information described above.
  • Google Gemini (AI service): receives your trip details, including any optional personal details, to create your itinerary. Under Google's terms for paid use, this information isn't used to improve Google's products. Google may keep it for a limited time to detect abuse.
  • Google Maps Platform and Apple Maps: receive the places you type or choose, including must-see places and a booked hotel, to suggest places, check that they exist, and plan routes.
  • Apple: processes your purchases and confirms them to us.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety or security of our users, the public or Tripzy.

We don't sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

5. International Transfers

Our service providers may process your information in countries other than your own, including the United States. Data protection laws there may differ from those where you live. Where the law requires it, these transfers are protected by safeguards such as the standard contractual clauses that our providers offer.

6. How Long We Keep Information

  • Trip details and generated plans: up to 24 hours on our servers, so that a retried request returns the same plan. Deletion can take up to one more day.
  • Credit and purchase records: as long as needed to provide the service, prevent double grants, resolve disputes, and meet accounting and legal requirements.
  • Security and usage-limit records: short periods only.
  • Feedback and support messages: as long as needed to respond and improve the App.
  • Trips saved in the App: on your device until you delete them or remove the App, subject to your device's backup settings.

Service providers keep information according to their own schedules, for example Google's limited retention of AI requests to detect abuse.

7. Your Choices and Rights

In the App, you can:

  • delete saved trips;
  • leave the optional personal details blank;
  • turn off anonymous usage totals;
  • turn off notifications in iOS Settings.

Your rights. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access your information, or receive a copy of it;
  • correct or delete it;
  • restrict or object to how we use it;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, which doesn't affect our earlier use;
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

To make a request, email us at the address below. Because Tripzy doesn't ask for your name or email address, we may need some details from the App on your device to find your information safely. We aim to reply within 30 days, and we won't treat you differently for using your rights. We may keep limited information where the law requires it, or for security, fraud prevention or accounting.

8. Children

Tripzy is for adults planning trips and isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under the minimum age set by the law where they live. The age range of a child traveling with you is used only to plan that trip. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We protect information with encrypted connections, checks that requests come from the genuine App, and strict limits on who can access our systems. No method of storing or sending information is completely secure, but we work to protect yours.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the App, our service providers or the law changes. We'll post the new version on this page with a new effective date. Where the law requires it, we'll also tell you in other ways.

11. Contact Us

The developer of the App is responsible for your information under this policy. For privacy questions or requests, email tripzy053@gmail.com.
