Privacy Policy

You can play offline without an account. The game does not send personal information, game progress, device identifiers, or usage records to the developer. It contains no ads, tracking, analytics SDKs, or third-party online services, and does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photos.

Local data and deletion

Delivery progress, best scores, unlocked letters, event stamps, and sound settings are stored locally in the app. The game does not provide cloud sync. System backups may retain data according to your Apple Account and device settings. To remove all local records, choose Delete App in your device settings; Offload App keeps documents and data. Manage backups separately in your system backup settings. The developer has no copy of your progress and cannot view or restore it remotely.

When you contact support

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use them only to reply, troubleshoot, or handle privacy requests, not for advertising or tracking. We retain emails only as long as needed to resolve the issue, follow up, or meet applicable obligations. You can request access, correction, or deletion from the email address you originally used. Support uses Gmail, whose provider handles email and backups under its own policies. Please do not send passwords or other unnecessary personal information.

Website and system services

The support website is hosted on GitHub Pages, with no ads, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies. GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. External websites have their own policies. Apple handles downloads, system diagnostics, and device backups under its policies and your settings. The game does not actively collect personal information from players of any age. Parents or guardians may contact us about support emails that children have sent voluntarily.

Contact and policy updates

Publisher name: Little Everyday Apps. Policy updates will be dated. If future features change how data is handled, we will update this policy accordingly.
