Make a little difference, one parcel at a time

Load your vans, plan the stops, and bring parcels to the neighbors of Qinghe Lane. Six chapters gently introduce fuller vans, shared routes, two-van teamwork, and a little detour.

Watch the neighborhood grow

Read letters from A-Kui, Auntie Lin, Xiao Zhou, and Xiao He. Your deliveries bring flowers to windowsills, chairs to the café, books to a reading nook, and a market to the street.

Play at your own pace

There is no countdown. Try optional errands, collect event stamps, and revisit delivery lists to find shorter routes. Enjoy original music and gentle everyday sound effects.

Made for quiet moments

For iPhone and iPad. English and Simplified Chinese. Offline play, no account, no ads, and no in-app purchases. Progress stays on your device.
