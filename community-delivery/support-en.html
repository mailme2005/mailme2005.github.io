Contact the developer

Email tripzy053@gmail.com. Please include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, game version, and the steps that led to the problem. Screenshots are optional. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information.

How do I make my first delivery?

Tap Make Your First Delivery, then Tap to load at the florist on the map, then Deliver to Florist. The van hands over the parcel automatically at the door. The round ends once every parcel is delivered and all vans have returned.

How do I plan a better route?

Tap the same neighbor again to load another parcel. Stops follow the order in which you first select neighbors. Before departing, use Edit Stops to move a stop earlier, compare the expected distance, or unload a parcel. Later chapters give you two vans, so you can load one while the other is out.

Are stars and errands required?

No. Deliver all parcels and return to the depot to progress through the six chapters. There is no countdown. Stars, trip badges, and event stamps are extra challenges. Errands count only after the qualifying parcels are delivered; finish the whole round to collect the stamp.

Where are the letters and neighborhood changes?

Tap the envelope at the top of the screen. Completing chapters unlocks replies from your neighbors and adds flowers, café seating, and a reading nook to the street. The events board features a flower swap, tea gathering, story time, or seed-sharing corner.

How do I adjust sound and animations?

Tap the sliders button to open Game Settings, then find Sound & Motion. Music and sound effects have separate switches and volume controls. You can also turn off animations. Sound respects Silent mode. Check your device volume and headphones. After disconnecting headphones, reopen settings and enable music or preview a sound to resume audio.

Do deliveries continue while I’m away?

No. The game saves and pauses when you leave. Tap Resume Deliveries when you return. Progress is stored on your device, with no account or cloud sync. Deleting the app removes its local data. The developer has no remote copy and cannot restore your progress.

How do I change the language?

Tap 中文 / English on the home screen, or open Game Settings with the sliders button. Choose 中文 or English at the top to switch instantly. Your language choice is saved, and your current delivery, progress, letters, stamps, and sound settings are kept. On first launch, the game follows your device’s preferred supported language, with English as the fallback.

Where can I read the privacy policy?

In Game Settings, scroll down to Help, Privacy & Music Credits. You can read the full policy offline. It is also available on this website.

Where do the music and artwork come from?

The background track, “A Slow Morning on Qinghe Lane,” and seven everyday sound effects were created for this project using original note arrangements and synthesized sound. No third-party songs, recordings, or samples were used. Neighbors, buildings, and vehicles are drawn in code; interface icons use Apple’s system symbols.
